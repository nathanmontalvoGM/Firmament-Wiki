Create a middle ground between the simplistic power scaling of DND and the rules bloated modifiers and bonuses of pathfinder. Retain a similar feat system for class progression as pathfinder, with variety of options at various levels. It should also be compatible in terminology with other DND 2014 and DND 2024 material for the purposes of monsters. 

Conditions should be simple, with consistent wording between all conditions to make interpreting them easy. 


**1\. How is your game different from the last uncountable number of dust collecting RPG releases from the last decade?**  
	It aims to have ease and teachability of DND with the more complex character customization of Pathfinder. The feats will picked during character creation are less specific and tailored to be more versatile and general with their application in the scope of specific class.
**2\. How is your game (different from/better than) similar games or games that inspired it?**  
	It wont have hard to keep track of bonuses and modifiers that, while mathematically balanced, don't make the player feel as powerful as traditional DND scaling. The system will reward team play and unlock new abilities for players  when they crit or a enemy reaches half HP.
3\. **Who is your target audience? What will they value in this game? What are the core game loops?** 
	Target audience is power gamers who want more flexibility with their characters and to have their choices rewarded in mechanical benefits. Players will value a similar feat progression system like pathfinder, but will more noticeable changes to their character in addition to more linear scaling in regards to their to hit bonuses and spell save DC's.
		Core game loops include
		beating a DC by 10
		using a special ability when a creature is bloodied
		improving a class feature by doing an additional effect when a condition or debuff is applied
		spending more actions on an ability to enhance its effects
		charegup mechanics to further enhance abilities, taking a negative for a positive later
**4\. How are the players supposed to feel when playing the game?**  
	like their choices matter and their character is powerful because of those choices. They should feel open to multiple options in combat and roleplay scenarios, not locked into one specific ability for their class
**5\. What are the PCs supposed to do? Not what CAN they do, what are they supposed to do? (reward these actions)**  
	Use teamwork, debuffs and strategy to win in combat scenarios. 
**6. What player behaviors do you find desirable in your game? (reward these actions)**  
	Creativity with abilities. I want my players to swing from a chandelier to gain an upper hand on the enemy. Or to use the environment in creative ways to defeat foes. This system should allow for that in every combat. Players should be able to create or describe objects in the scene to help them, especially if the GM didn't think of it prior. 
**7\. What kinds of play styles and genre(s) does your game cater to? What is the intended atmosphere?**  
	traditional wizards and fighter DND style fantasy. 
**8\. Who has what control of what part of the narrative and when?**  
	The DM has control over setting the scene, but players should be able to create objects or alter the world based on their characters.
**9\. How do your intended resolution mechanics and sub systems reinforce the game’s world building and intended play experience?**  
	Linear scaling bonuses allow for previously challenging DC's to become trivial, allowing characters to surmount challenge catered to their character easier. 
**10\. What areas of your game deserve extra development and attention to enhance the intended play experience?**
	Combat. This system is designed to be combat heavy and easy to run. Conditions, actions and abilities should be intuitive and easy to understand at a glance with consistent wording between descriptions
