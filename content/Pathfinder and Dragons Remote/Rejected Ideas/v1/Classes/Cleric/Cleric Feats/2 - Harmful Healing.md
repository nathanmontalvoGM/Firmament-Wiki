**Prerequisite:** [[Life Domain]] 
**Level:** 2
**Tag:** #Radiant

Your deity has granted you the power to use your healing offensively. When you would cast a spell to restore hit points on a creature, you can instead choose to deal damage instead, dealing radiant damage. You can cast spells in this way a number of times equal to your spellcasting ability(minimum of 1)
