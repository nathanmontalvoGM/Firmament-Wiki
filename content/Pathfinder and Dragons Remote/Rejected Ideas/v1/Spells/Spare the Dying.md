- **Tradition:** #DivineTradition 
- **Casting Time:** 2-3 actions
- **Range:** touch
**Targets:** A living creature that has 0 hit points
- **Components:** #Vitality #necromancy 
- **Casting Requirements:** #Verbal #Somatic 
- **Duration:** #Instantaneous
**Stability:** #Stable 

**2 actions:** You touch a living creature that has 0 hit points. The creature becomes stable. This spell has no effect on undead or constructs.
**3 actions:** The spell has a range of 30 feet.
