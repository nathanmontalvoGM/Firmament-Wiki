## Keystone Levelling

### brainstorm
instead of an XP based system or milestone, PAD will use Keystone based leveling (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2BYv5F541PY&t=728s).

Players slowly fill boxes in a chart with each box representing a keystone.

Keystones could be quests or character backstory rewards. Players should always be informed of what grants them a keystone so levelling up is less random. 

## Keystone Levelling

Each character has a level chart containing a series of boxes. Filling these boxes advances your character toward the next level. Players always know what fills each box (quests or objectives).

### Secondary Bonuses
Once per level, players can forgo getting a Keystone level and instead gain a permeant bonus to their characters. Pick from the list below.

- **Saving Throw Training:** Gain training in one saving throw you aren't already proficient in
- **Skill Training:** Gain training in one skill you aren't already proficient in.
- **Battle Hardened:** Gain a permanent +1 bonus to AC
- **Resilient:** Your hit point maximum increases by your Constitution modifier
- **Swift:** Your movement speed permanently increases by 5 feet
