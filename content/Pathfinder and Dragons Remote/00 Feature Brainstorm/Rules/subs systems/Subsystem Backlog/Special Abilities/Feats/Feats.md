## What Comprises a Feat?

- **Prerequisite:** what class, subclass or tag is required before taking this feat
- **Level:** the required level before taking this feat
- **Tag:** what elements comprise this feat
- **Type:** #FlexibleFeat or #RudimentaryFeat . Flexible feats allow you to use a [[Source]] when selecting the feat. Rudimentary Feats do not.

- **Target:** how many creatures the spell affects
- **Range:** the scope of the feat measured in feat. Creatures outside of its range cannot be targeted.
- **Action Cost:** how many actions the feat takes to use. Feats marked With —- have no action cost
- **Duration:** how long the feats effects last for

**Fundamental:** the core of the feat, the effects provided that never change regardless of training

*Training:* How proficient you are with this feature. You automatically start at your feats lowest training proficiency and gain new training proficiencies as you level up.
