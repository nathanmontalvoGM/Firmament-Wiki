
# Strength Requirements
Most armor requires a base level of physical strength to wear and use effectively. This will be listed by the armors **Strength Requirement.** If you do not meet the strength requirement you can still wear the armor but gain the following penalties.
* -10ft. speed penalty (minimum of 5ft).
* You are [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Hindered]] on all Strength and Dexterity based d20 rolls unless you are [[Attacking]].
