[[Hit Points]] represent a character's life and blood, and whether they have taken any wounds. You’re starting [[Hit Points]] are determined by taking your [[Maximum Health]] and subtracting your [[Stamina]]. The remaining total are your [[Hit Points]].

If you’re a Barbarian and you start with 15 [[Maximum Health]] and 2 [[Stamina]], you’re starting [[Hit Points]] would be 13. 
You don’t start removing hit points till you’re out of [[Stamina]]. When you start removing hit points you gain the [[01 Condition#Bloodied (WIP)|Bloodied]] condition. When you drop to 0, you are [[01 Condition#Unconscious|Unconscious]]
