## Idea 1
Instead of rolling a d20 and taking the higher value, instead you roll 1d10 and add it to your roll. This means you will at minimum be getting a +2 to your roll which is equivalent to a critical aid check in pf2e

Hindered works the same but is subtracted from your roll

If you are hindered to other creatures you roll 1d10 and subtract it from your roll

if you have Favored, you roll 1d10 and add it to your roll

Players should be encouraged to describe how their PC gains Favored with this. 

## Idea 2
instead of rolling a d20 and taking the higher value, roll 1d6 for each stack of Favored or Hindered and add it to your roll.

If you are hindered on your attack rolls and hindered on your ability checks, that is considered two stacks of hindered meaning both of those rolls now have 2d6 subtracted from them. You can only have a maximum of two Favored or Hinderance on your character at one time. 

If you have just one stack of Hindered, it cancels out all stacks of Favored. 

NOTE: Hindered and Favored would be the only stacking conditions in the game which might be a design contradiction.
