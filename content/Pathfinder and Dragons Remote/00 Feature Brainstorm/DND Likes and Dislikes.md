# Likes
Simplicity and ease of learning
# Dislikes
* Linear class progression. You pick your race, class and subclass and outside of optional feats, are done with your character. 
* action, BA, RA and Move. many times i feel like i waste part of my turn not being able to use a BA
* Power scaling at levels 10 and beyond make the game feel unwieldly for both me as DM and a player. 
* Effects like stun that remove your player agency are unfun for both DM and player. No one wants their turn removed
