# Likes
Customizable classes with feats 
Unique class features and classes. Doesn't feel like "generic fantasy"
3 action system
Spell scaling. Spells have different effects with 1-3 actions. I would want more of these types of spells 
Concentration being an action instead of just a roll when you get hit
having a set of DC's so you always know what number you are supposed to be at
You're numbers always going up. A level 1 pf2e character feels vastly weaker than a level 10 does in terms of its to hit bonus for example
+10/-10 crit. More crits is always more fun
More options in combat then just attacking 
Proficiency system (trained, expert etc.)
having up to 3 inspiration
The traits system makes identifying what abilities do easier. Although there might be too many traits in pathfinder 
# Dislikes
Too many incremental bonus for characters, making levelling up feel less impactful vs. DND
Circumstance, status and item bonuses don't feel as impactful as something like advantage.
Too many abilities are very situational and don't feel impactful 90% of the time
Magic items don't make much of an impact, again only providing bonuses which feel worse as a player
having to give inspiration every hour
