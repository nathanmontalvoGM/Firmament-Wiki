# Level 2+
## Iron Discipline
Your training overrides pain and exhaustion.

**Trigger:** While you are not [[01 Condition#Bloodied (WIP)|Bloodied]]:

**Trained**: Gain a +2 bonus to AC.
**Expert**: Gain a +2 bonus to AC and saving throws.
**Master**: Gain a +2 bonus to AC, saving throws, and attack rolls.
**Legendary**: Gain a +4 bonus to AC, saving throws, and attack rolls
