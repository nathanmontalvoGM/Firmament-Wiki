- **Prerequisite:** [[Dueling Discipline]]
- **Level:** 4
- **Tag:** #Strike #Maneuver 
- **Type:** #FlexibleFeat 
- **Trigger:** You are attacking your Duel Target.
- **Cost:** 1 Action
**Fundamental:**- 
Make a melee attack. 

- **Trained:** On a hit, your target cannot gain [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]] until the start of your next turn.
- **Expert:** As Trained. They cannot remove conditions until the start of your next turn.
- **Master:** As Expert. Your next attack doesn't incur [[Multiple Attack Penalty]].
