- **Prerequisite:** [[Dueling Discipline]]
- **Level:** 2
- **Tag:** #Haste #Strike #Maneuver
- **Type:** #FlexibleFeat 
- **Trigger:** You are attacking your Duel Target.
- **Cost:** 1 action
**Fundamental:**
Make a melee attack. On a hit you may reposition yourself 5 feet without provoking [[Opportunity Attack]]s and your target becomes [[01 Condition#Off-Guard|Off-Guard]] to your attacks until the start of your next turn.

- **Trained:** As above. The target is [[01 Condition#Off-Guard|Off-Guard]] to your attacks only.
- **Expert:** As Trained. The reposition increases to 10 feet. The target is [[01 Condition#Off-Guard|Off-Guard]] to all attacks until the start of your next turn.
- **Master:** As Expert. The reposition increases to 15 feet.
