- **Prerequisite:** [[Dueling Discipline]]
- **Level:** 4
- **Tag:** #Strike #Maneuver 
- **Type:** #FlexibleFeat 
- **Trigger:** You are attacking your Duel Target and they are [[01 Condition#Bloodied (WIP)|Bloodied]].
- **Cost:** 1 Action

You strike with careful precision, targeting your opponent’s exposed wounds. Make a melee attack. 

- **Trained:** On Hit, the target takes 1d4 persistent bleed damage.
- **Expert:** The target takes 2d4 persistent bleed damage. Additionally the target must make a Constitution saving throw against your Class DC or take a −2 penalty to attack rolls until they are no longer [[01 Condition#Bloodied (WIP)|Bloodied]].
- **Master:** The target takes 3d4 persistent bleed damage. While the target is suffering persistent bleed damage from this feat, they cannot remove the [[01 Condition#Bloodied (WIP)|Bloodied]] condition even if they are healed past half their [[Hit Points]].
