- **Prerequisite:** [[Dueling Discipline]]
- **Level:** 6
- **Tag:** #Strike #Steadfast #Maneuver 
- **Type:** #FlexibleFeat 
- **Trigger:** The creature is your Duel Target and triggers an [[Opportunity Attack]].
- **Cost:** 1 Reaction
Make a melee attack. On a hit, the target must make a Strength saving throw vs your Class DC.

- **Expert:** On a failure, they have their movement speed reduced to 0 until the end of their next turn.
- **Master:** As Expert. While their movement speed is reduced to 0 all Dexterity saving throws are [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Hindered]].
