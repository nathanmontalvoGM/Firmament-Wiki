- **Prerequisite:** [[Dueling Discipline]]
- **Level:** 2
- **Tag:** #Aegis #Maneuver 
- **Type:** #FlexibleFeat 
- **Cost:** 1 Reaction

**Fundamental:**
When another creature would damage you with a melee attack, you can use your reaction to increase your AC by +2, possibly turning a hit into a miss. If the attack misses, you can attempt to [[Disarm]] the target as part of the reaction.

- **Trained:** Gain +2 AC against the triggering attack. On a miss you may attempt to [[Disarm]] the attacker.
- **Expert:** As Trained. If the attack still hits despite the bonus, you reduce the damage taken by an amount equal to your Strength or Dexterity modifier (Whichever is higher). 
- **Master:** As Expert. The AC bonus increases to +4. On a successful [[Disarm]], you can choose to grab the weapon if you have a free hand to do so. You can attempt to wield the weapon (if you are at least trained with the weapons type).
