- **Prerequisite:** [[Dueling Discipline]] #Strike2 
- **Level:** 8
- **Tag:** #Strike #Fortune #Maneuver 
- **Type:** #FleibleFeat 
- **Trigger:** You miss your Duel Target with a melee attack
- **Cost:** Reaction
**Fundamental:**
Reroll the attack. 

- **Expert:** If the reroll hits, you may immediately make a second melee attack after resolving the first. This attack occurs [[Multiple Attack Penalty]] only after the attack is made. 
- **Master:** As Expert. If the second attack hits, it deals two additional [[Weapon Dice]] of damage.
