- **Prerequisite:** [[Dueling Discipline]]
- **Level:** 4
- **Tag:** #Aegis #Maneuver 

**Cost:** 1 Reaction

When another creature would damage you with a melee attack, you can use your reaction to increase your AC by +2, possibly turning a hit into a miss. If the attack misses, you can attempt to disarm the target as part of the reaction.
