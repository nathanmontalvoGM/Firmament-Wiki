- **Prerequisite:** [[Dueling Discipline]]
- **Level:** 2
- **Tag:** #Strike #Maneuver 
- **Type:** #FlexibleFeat 
- **Trigger:** You are attacking your Duel Target.
- **Cost:** 1 action

**Fundamental:**
Make a melee attack. On a hit you deal additional [[Weapon Dice|Weapon Die]] of damage determined by your training.

- **Trained:** Deal 1 additional [[Weapon Dice|Weapon Die]] of damage. This bonus only applies on the first attack you make against your Duel Target each turn.
- **Expert:** Deal 2 additional [[Weapon Dice]] of damage. If this attack is made with [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]], the target also becomes [[01 Condition#Off-Guard|Off-Guard]] to your attacks until the start of your next turn.
- **Master:** Deal 3 additional [[Weapon Dice]] of damage. If this attack causes the target to gain the [[01 Condition#Bloodied (WIP)|Bloodied]] condition, you may immediately make one additional attack against them as a free action. This attack applies [[Multiple Attack Penalty]] as normal.
