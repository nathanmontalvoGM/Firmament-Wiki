- **Prerequisite:** [[Dueling Discipline]]
- **Level:** 6
- **Tag:** #Aegis #Maneuver 
- **Type:** #FlexibleFeat 
- **Cost:** 2 Actions

**Fundamental:**
You brace yourself for an incoming assault, reading your Duel Target's movements and preparing your defenses. Until the start of your next turn you gain a bonus to AC and Strength and Dexterity saving throws. 

- **Expert:** Gain a +4 bonus to AC and Strength and Dexterity saving throws. Treat the highest damage die of any attack that hits you as a 1. Additionally if your Duel Target misses you while this effect is active, you may immediately move up to 10 feet as a free action without provoking [[Opportunity Attack]]s
- **Master:** As Expert. The bonus increases to +6. Treat the two highest damage dice of any attack that hits you as 1s.
