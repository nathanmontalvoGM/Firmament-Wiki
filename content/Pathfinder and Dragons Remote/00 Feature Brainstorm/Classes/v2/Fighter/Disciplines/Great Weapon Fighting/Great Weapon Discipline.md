You specialize in two-handed weapons and powerful strikes. When you select this discipline at level 1, you gain the Vicious Swing feature.
## Weapon Specialization
Weapons that are two-handed or versatile. If you select a versatile weapon, it must be wielded two-handed to gain your Discipline's features.

## Core Subclass Feature
* Two weapon fighting is about landing really big impactful attacks.
* Maybe cleaving attacks 
* High risk high reward 
### Idea 1
Once Per turn, you can turn any attack into a Great Swing. You must decide this before making the attack. Doing so gives you an -2 penalty to the roll, and increases the amount of [[Weapon Dice]] you roll determined by your training in this feat. You reroll 1s and 2s on any [[Weapon Dice]] made with the attack. You must use the new result. 

If this attack fails, you are [[01 Condition#Off-Guard|Off-Guard]] until the start of your next turn. 

**Trained:** 1 [[[[Weapon Dice]]]]
**Expert:**  2 [[[[Weapon Dice]]]]
**Master:** 4 [[[[Weapon Dice]]]]
**Legendary:** 6 [[[[Weapon Dice]]]]
##### Note
Might increase action cost of this attack by 1.
