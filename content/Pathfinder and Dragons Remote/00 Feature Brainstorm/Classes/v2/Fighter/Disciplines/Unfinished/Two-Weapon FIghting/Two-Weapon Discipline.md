You specialize in relentless pressure and speed, wielding two-handed weapons. When you select this discipline at level 1, you gain the Relentless Assault feature.

## Weapon Specialization
Two-handed weapons.
## Relentless Assault
2 action

Make a melee attack against a creature you can see. If you hit, immediately roll a second attack. This second attack gains a +2 bonus to damage rolls. This attack occurs multiple attack penalty only after both attacks are made. If you hit a creature with both weapons in the same turn, you create an opening.
