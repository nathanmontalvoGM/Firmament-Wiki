You specialize in ranged combat and forcing enemies into cover. When you select this discipline at level 1, you gain the Punish Movement feature.

# Weapon Specialization 
Two-handed ranged martial weapons. Firearms cannot benefit from this disciplines features.
# Punish Movement (WIP)
1 Reaction

When a creature moves at least 10 feet while within your weapon’s first range increment, you create an opening. If the movement was voluntary, you may immediately use a maneuver as a reaction. If you use a maneuver in this way, you gain a +2 to damage rolls with your weapon.
