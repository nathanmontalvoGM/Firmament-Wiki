# Class Identity
## What is the core fantasy of this class?
* Specializing in fighting styles and excelling in those specific styles of fighting. Should excel in being exceptionally strong or agile.
## What does this class do better than any other?
* Versatility. This class should be able to built a dozen ways while being unique in each playstyle. A fighter should be able to be built for any situation, allowing for a theoretical all fighter party. Jack of all trades masters of all.

## What emotions or vibes should players feel while playing this class?
**In Combat:** Like they can make adapt to any situation or be the best at one thing
**In Roleplay:** 
## What problems does this class solve for the party?
* Can fill any gaps in a party. Healer, rudimentary spellcaster, tank.
## What should this class _never_ be allowed to do?
* Be the best option for a spellcaster or healer. A fighter CAN do these things, but he shouldn't be the goto option. 
# Core Mechanics
## What is the class’s signature resource or mechanic?

* Discipline 
	* What kind of fighter are you. Your maneuvers are modified by the discipline you select. For example, an archer can gain special maneuvers to use with a bow, while a great weapon fighter would have different maneuvers. 
* Maneuvers
	* The ways a fighter can use weapons within their discipline. These manuevers require a certain prerec to met (such as, they are [[Bloodied]] or [[01 Condition#Off-Guard|Off-Guard]])
	* gets a maneuver back on crit
## What does this resource encourage players to do?
* Switch stances when optimal. Certain stance modes should be able to gain maneuvers back when they do something successfully.
