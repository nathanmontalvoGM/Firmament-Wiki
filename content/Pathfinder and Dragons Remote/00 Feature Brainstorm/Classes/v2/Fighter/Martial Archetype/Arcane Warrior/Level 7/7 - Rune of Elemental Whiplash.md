Flavor Text.

## Greater Rune
On hit, the creature takes an extra 2d8 elemental damage (choose acid, cold, fire, or electricity when you inscribe the rune). The next time the creature hits you before the start of your next turn, it takes 1d8 damage of the same type.

## Master Rune
As Greater Rune, but the reactive damage increases to 2d8 and applies even if the creature hits another target.
