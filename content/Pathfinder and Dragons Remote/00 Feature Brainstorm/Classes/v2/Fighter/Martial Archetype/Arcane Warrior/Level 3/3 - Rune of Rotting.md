**Prerequisite:** [[Arcane Warrior]]
**Level:** 3
**Tag:** #Rune 

Your attacks are coated with a magical noxious odor.

# # Lesser Rune
On hit, the creature takes an extra 1d8 poison damage. The target must also succeed on a Constitution saving throw against your Class DC, or become [[01 Condition#Sickened|Sickened]].
# Greater Rune
As Lesser Rune, but the damage increases to 2d8. 
# Master Rune
As Greater Rune, but any creature other than you that starts its turn within 15ft. of targeted creature must also succeed on a Constitution saving throw against your Class DC, or become [[01 Condition#Sickened|Sickened]].
