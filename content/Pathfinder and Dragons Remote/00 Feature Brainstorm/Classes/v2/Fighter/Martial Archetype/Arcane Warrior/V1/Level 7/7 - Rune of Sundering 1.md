- **Prerequisite:** [[Arcane Warrior]]
- **Level:** 7
- **Tag:** #Rune 

Your attacks exploit weakness in your opponents armor.

## Greater Rune

On hit, the creature takes an extra 2d8 force damage. Until the start of your next turn, the creature is [[01 Condition#Off-Guard|Off-Guard]].

## Master Rune
As Greater Rune, but the creature gains weakness equal to your level to your next attack until the end of your next turn.
