## Feats Per Level

## Intro
You have enchanted your weapons with powerful runes to enhance your combat abilities. 

## Arcane Runes
At 3rd level, you learn to unleash special magical effects with some of your attacks. As you level up in this class, you gain access to runes you can apply to a weapon you have expert proficiency or greater with.  As you level up, your existing runes become more powerful as listed in the runes entry. You also can apply more runes to your weapon as you level up but only one runes effects can be activated per turn. Unless otherwise stated, your runes are activates as part of the attack. Runes can be swapped and replaced during your [[daily preparations]].

| Level | Rune Tier    | Active Runes |
| ----- | ------------ | ------------ |
| 3     | Lesser Rune  | 1            |
| 7     | Greater Rune | 2            |
| 10    | Master Rune  | 3            |
- You may only have one rune active per weapon.
- If you wield multiple weapons, only one weapon can be rune-bound at a time unless a feat says otherwise.
- Higher-tier runes replace lower-tier runes on that weapon (i.e. Greater Rune replacing Lesser Rune).
## Weapons
**Ranged:** The rune is applied to your bow. All non-magical arrows inherit the runes effects but magical arrows do not.
**Melee:** Nonmagical weapons inherit the runes properties, magical weapons do not.

## Magic Bypass
At 7th level, your attacks count as magical for the sake of overcoming resistance and immunity to nonmagical attacks and damage.

## Guided Attack
At 10th level, you learn how to use magic to aid your attacks. When you make an attack roll with an enchanted weapon and miss, you can reroll the attack.
