## Feats Per Level

## Intro
You have enchanted your weapons with powerful runes to enhance your combat abilities. 

## [[Arcane Rune Feats]]
A complete list of all feats for Arcane Warrior
## Arcane Runes
At 3rd level, you learn to unleash special magical effects with some of your attacks. As you level up in this class, you gain access to runes you can apply to your Signature Weapon. You unlock these runes as feats as you gains levels in this archetype. Unless otherwise stated, your runes are activates as part of the attack and you can only use one of the active runes you have attuned to your Signature Weapon. Runes can be swapped and replaced during your [[daily preparations]].

## Weapons
**Ranged:** The rune is applied to your bow. All non-magical arrows inherit the runes effects but magical arrows do not.
**Melee:** Nonmagical weapons inherit the runes properties, magical weapons do not.

## Magic Bypass
At 7th level, your attacks count as magical for the sake of overcoming resistance and immunity to nonmagical attacks and damage.

## Guided Attack
At 10th level, you learn how to use magic to aid your attacks. When you make an attack roll with an enchanted weapon and miss, you can reroll the attack once per turn.
