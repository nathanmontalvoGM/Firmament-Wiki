# Level Three
## Openings
Like super sick and awesome flavor text. Weapon masters looks at combat and tries to exploit their enemies small mistakes to their advantage. When you gain this archetype at level 3, you gain access to the following openings. These openings allow you to use Combat Exploits as a result of gaining an opening.

The rules for openings are as follows:
- An opening lasts until the end of your current turn.
- If an opening occurs outside your turn (e.g., an enemy misses you), it lasts until the end of your next turn. 
- If you do not use an exploit during an opening, then the opening is lost.

A weapon master can exploit a variety of weakness in an enemy. You start with following ways to gain an opening, and will unlock more as you gain levels in this subclass. 

You may only exploit an opening once per turn.
### Level 3
- You critically hit a creature
- The creature is [[01 Condition#Off-Guard|Off-Guard]].
### Level 7
- You force a creature to fail a saving throw.
- A creature misses you with an attack

## Combat Exploits
Once per turn, when a creature within range of your weapon creates an Opening for you, you deal additional damage equal to your training with that weapon. In addition you also use one of your Combat Exploit feats. You can use any of the Combat Exploit feats you have available, as long as you meet the **Trigger** requirements the feat lists.
 
You select an additional Combat Exploit feat at level 3, and again at level 7 and 10. Combat Exploit feats have the #CombatExploit tag associated with them. 

# Level 7
## Versatile Discipline 
You can select feats from other fighting styles. You ignore subclass prerequisites when selecting these feats but the feats level, class and tags still apply. You gain A feat of your choice from that Discipline as long as you meet its prerequisites and that subclasses 1st level feature.
