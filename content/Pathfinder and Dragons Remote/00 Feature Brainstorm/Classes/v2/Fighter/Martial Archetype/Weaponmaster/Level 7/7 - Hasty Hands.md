- **Prerequisite:** #Fighter [[Weaponmaster]]
- **Level:** 7
- **Tag:** #Haste #CombatExploit 
- **Type:** #RudimentaryFeat 

**Trigger:** You make an attack with a weapon you are proficient with.
**Fundamental:** Your strikes control the rhythm of battle. When you hit a creature with a triggering attack, you gain an additional action that must be used immediately. This action can only be used to perform one of the actions listed for your training level.
- **Expert:** [[interact]], [[Reload]]
- **Master:** [[Take Cover]], [[Raise Shield]]
- **Legendary:** [[Move]]
