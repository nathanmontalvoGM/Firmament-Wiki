**Prerequisite:** #Fighter [[Weaponmaster]]
**Level:** 3
**Tag:** #Aegis #CombatExploit 
**Type:** #RudimentaryFeat 

**Trigger:** You make an attack with a weapon you are proficient with.
**Fundamental:** After hitting with an attack, you gain a bonus to your AC determined by your training until the start of your next turn.

**Trained:** +2 AC. 
**Expert:**  +3 AC. You gain resistance 3 to the attack.
**Master:** +4 AC. You gain resistance 5 to the attack. If the target is within range of your weapon, you can make an [[Opportunity Attack]] as a reaction.
**Legendary:** +5 AC. You gain resistance 7 to the attack. The [[Opportunity Attack]] gains  [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]]
