# Class Identity
## What is the core fantasy of this class?
Armored priest that provides healing, utility and buffs to themselves and others granted from their deity. 

## What does this class do better than any other?
Be a pseudo frontline with high AC for a spell caster while dealing high damage with spells or melee attacks with their favored weapon.

## What emotions or vibes should players feel while playing this class?
**In Combat** Tanky and Powerful buffing and healing others while casting damaging spells
**In Roleplay:** Knowledgeable and wise in matter relating to their deity. 

## What problems does this class solve for the party?
Tanking and Support 

## What should this class _never_ be allowed to do?
Have high mobility, apply status debuffs to enemies.
