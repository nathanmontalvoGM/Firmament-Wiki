**Prerequisite:** [[Life Domain]], #Vitality3, #Strike1 
**Level:** 10
**Tag:** #Vitality 
**Type:** #FlexibleFeat 

**Source:** 1 of any source (minor, major or severe)

**Behavior:** 1 of any behavior (minor, major or severe)

Your healing invigorates your allies. When you cast a spell that restores hit points to a single target and that target regains [[Hit Points]] as a result of that spell, you can choose to quicken that creature until the end of their next turn. While quickened in this way, the creature gains the following benefits:

 * +15 **Flat Modifier** to movement speed, 
 * all their attacks gain [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]] 
 * you add your spellcasting ability to their damage rolls as a **Flat Modifier**.
