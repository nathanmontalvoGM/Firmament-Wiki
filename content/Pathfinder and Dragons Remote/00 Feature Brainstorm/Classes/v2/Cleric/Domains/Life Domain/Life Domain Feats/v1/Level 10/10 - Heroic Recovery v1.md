**Prerequisite:** [[Life Domain]], #Vitality3
**Level:** 10
**Tag:** #Vitality 

Your healing invigorates those you cast it on. When you cast a spell that restores hit points to a single target and that target regains [[Hit Points]] as a result of that spell, you can choose to quicken that creature until the end of their next turn. While quickened in this way, the creature gains a +10 bonus to movement speed, all their attacks gain [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]]and you add your spellcasting ability to their damage rolls.
