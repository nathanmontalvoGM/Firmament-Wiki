**Prerequisite:** [[Life Domain]], #Vitality3, #Aegis1 
**Level:** 10
**Tag:** #Vitality #Aegis

Your healing provides temporary protection to those you cast it on. When you cast a spell that restores hit points to a single target other than yourself and that target regains [[Hit Points]] as a result of that spell, you add your spellcasting ability to that creature's AC and all Saving Throws until the end of their next turn.
