Prerequisite:** [[Life Domain]], #Aegis3
**Level:** 10
**Tag:** #Vitality 

**Frequency:** Once per hour
**Duration:** concentration up to 1 minute

For 1 minute, as long as you have at least 1 Essence, all healing you receive is rolled at its maximum value. In addition, if any ally takes damage within range of your Weave Essence domain feature, you receive half the damage as healing.

You lose 1 Essence at the end of each of your turns.
