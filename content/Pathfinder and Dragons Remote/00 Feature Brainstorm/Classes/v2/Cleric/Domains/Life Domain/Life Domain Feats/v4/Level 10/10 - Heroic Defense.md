- **Prerequisite:** [[Life Domain]], #Vitality3, #Aegis1 
- **Level:** 10
- **Tag:** #Vitality #Aegis
- **Type:** #FlexibleFeat 

**Source:** 1 of any source (minor, major or severe)

**Behavior:** 1 of any behavior (minor, major or severe)

Your healing provides temporary protection to those you cast it on. When you cast a spell that restores hit points to a single target other than yourself and that target regains [[Hit Points]] as a result of that spell, the target gains the following benefits:

+5 **Flat Modifier** to AC and all [[saving throws]] until the end of their next turn. 

**Inherited:** Sources and Behaviors applied to this feat apply to the spell its used with.
