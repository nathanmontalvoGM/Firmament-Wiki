**Prerequisite:** [[Life Domain]] 
**Level:** 4
**Tag:** #Radiant  #Strike

You gain the ability to infuse your weapon strikes with divine energy. Once on each of your turns when you hit a creature with your favored weapon, you can cause the attack to deal an extra 1d8 radiant damage to the target.
