- **Prerequisite:** [[Cleric]], [[Martyr Domain]] #Strike1 , #Vitality1 
- **Level:** 6
- **Tag:** #Strike 
- **Type:** #FlexibleFeat 

- **Target:** 1 creature
- **Range:** range of your melee weapon
- **Action Cost:** 2
- **Duration:** #Instantaneous 
**Fundamental:**
Make a melee weapon attack. On a hit, the target must make a Strength saving throw against your spell save DC or become [[01 Condition#Weakened (Strength)|Weakened]]. While [[01 Condition#Weakened (Strength)|Weakened]] in this way, allies who damage the target can choose to receive [[Greater and Lesser Healing|Greater Healing]] equal to your training level. You take damage equal to your training level each time this healing occurs. This damage cannot be reduced in any way. The target repeats this saving throw at the end of each of their turns.

- **Expert:** 2d8 [[Greater and Lesser Healing|Greater Healing]], 1d6 radiant damage
- **Master:** 4d8 [[Greater and Lesser Healing|Greater Healing]], 2d6 radiant damage
