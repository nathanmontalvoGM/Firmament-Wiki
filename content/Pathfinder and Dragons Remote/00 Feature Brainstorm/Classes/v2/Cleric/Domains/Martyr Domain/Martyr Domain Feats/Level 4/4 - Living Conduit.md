- **Prerequisite:** [[Cleric]], [[Martyr Domain]]
- **Level:** 4
- **Tag:** #Vitality #Strike 
- **Type:** #RudimentaryFeat 

- **Target:** 1 creature
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Action Cost:** ---
- **Duration:** #Instantaneous 
- **Trigger:** You are targeted by an effect that restores [[Greater and Lesser Healing|Greater Healing]].

**Fundamental:**
Instead of regaining [[Hit Points]] from the effect, you absorb the healing into you, redirecting it to others. The effect that would heal you rolls double its amount of healing dice. This healing is then stored as a pool of healing for later use. You can use this reserve of healing in two ways:
* You draw from the pool of healing and add it to your next #Vitality spell as [[Greater and Lesser Healing|Greater Healing]].
* You draw from the pool of healing and add it to your next spell as radiant damage. Doing so deals half the damage back to you. This damage cannot be reduced in any way.
