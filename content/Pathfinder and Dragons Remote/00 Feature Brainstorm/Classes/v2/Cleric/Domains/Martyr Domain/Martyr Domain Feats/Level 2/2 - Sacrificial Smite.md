- **Prerequisite:** [[Cleric]], [[Martyr Domain]]
- **Level:** 2
- **Tag:** #Strike 
- **Type:** #FlexibleFeat 

- **Target:** 1 creature hit with a melee attack
- **Range:** melee
- **Action Cost:** ---
- **Duration:** #Instantaneous 

**Fundamental:**
When you hit a creature with a melee attack, you may spend up to 5 [[Hit Points]] to deal additional radiant damage equal to the [[Hit Points]] spent plus an amount determined by your training. This damage increase is doubled while you are [[Bloodied]].

- **Trained:** +3
- **Expert:** +6
- **Master:** +9
