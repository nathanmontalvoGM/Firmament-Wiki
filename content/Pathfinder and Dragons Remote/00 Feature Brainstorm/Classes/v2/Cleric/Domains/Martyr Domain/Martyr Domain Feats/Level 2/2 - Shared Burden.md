- **Prerequisite:** [[Cleric]], [[Martyr Domain]]
- **Level:** 2
- **Tag:** #Aegis 
- **Type:** #FlexibleFeat 

- **Target:** 1 creature within range
- **Range:** 30 feet.
- **Action Cost:** 1 reaction
- **Duration:** #Instantaneous 
- **Trigger:** An ally within 30 feet takes damage.

**Fundamental:** 
As a reaction, you may take up to half the triggering damage yourself, reducing the damage the ally takes by that amount. If you are [[Bloodied]] when you use this feature, the ally also gains temporary Hit Points equal to the damage you absorbed.
