**Fundamental:**
When you spend [[Hit Points]] using Blood Offering, you may add your Wisdom modifier to the next saving throw you make before the start of your next turn. 

- **Trained:** You and one ally within 30 feet gain a bonus to your next saving throw equal to your wisdom modifier.
- **Expert:** You and up to two allies within 30 feet gain the bonus.
- **Master:** All allies within 30 feet gain the bonus. If an ally succeeds on a saving throw while benefiting from this effect, they regain Hit Points equal to your Wisdom modifier.
