- **Prerequisite:** [[Bard]]
- **Level:** 6
- **Tag:** #PerformingArt 
- **Type:** #FlexibleFeat 

- **Target:** 1 creature 
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Action Cost:** 1 Reaction 
- **Duration:** #Instantaneous 

**Fundamental:** Flavor text. As a reaction you attempt to counter a spell you can see within range. You expend a number of [[Mana]] equal to the [[Mana]] the enemy spellcaster spent on their spell. You then roll a performance check vs the Constitution DC of the creature. 

**Critical Success:** You redirect the spell to another creature within the spells range. You cast the spell as if it was in your prepared spell list, rolling a spell attack or forcing another creature to make a spell save as normal. You use the same Source and Delivery the original spell used but without expending its [[Mana]] cost.
**Success:** The spell is wasted.

- **Expert:** You get a critical success on an 18, 19, and 20 when rolling your performance check. 
- **Master:** The performance check is always [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]]. [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Hindered]] still cancels out [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]] as normal.
