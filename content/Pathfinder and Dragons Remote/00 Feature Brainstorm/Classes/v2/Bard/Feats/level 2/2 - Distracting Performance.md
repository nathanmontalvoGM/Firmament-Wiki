- **Prerequisite:** [[Bard]]
- **Level:** 2
- **Tag:** #PerformingArt 
- **Type:** #FlexibleFeat 

- **Range:** 30 foot aura 

**Fundamental:** 
You create a performance specifically targeting a creature you can see, Choose a creature you can see within range. You start a performance designed to distract or annoy that target. The target must make a Wisdom saving throw or take a penalty to all their d20 rolls based on your training in this feat as long as they are within the range of your Distracting Performance. The creature can repeat this save at the end of their turn as long as they are not within the Distracting Performance. 

- **Trained:** -2 penalty
- **Expert:** -4 penalty
- **Master:** -6 penalty
