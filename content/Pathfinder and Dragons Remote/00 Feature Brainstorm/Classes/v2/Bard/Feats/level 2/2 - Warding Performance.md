- **Prerequisite:** [[Bard]]
- **Level:** 2
- **Tag:** #Aegis #PerformingArt 
- **Type:** #FlexibleFeat 

**Fundamental:** 
Flavor text. Allies that start or enter their turn within 30 feet and can see or hear your Courageous Performance gain the [[01 Condition#Inspired|Inspired]] condition.​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​ While [[01 Condition#Inspired|Inspired]], If they are hit with an attack within the next minute, they reduce the damage of that attack by an amount determined by your training. They then lose the [[01 Condition#Inspired|Inspired]] condition. 

- **Trained:** 1d6
- **Expert:** 2d6 and the ally gains resistance to that damage type equal to the Bards level until the bards stops their Warding Performance. 
- **Master:** 3d6, you reroll 1s and 2s.
