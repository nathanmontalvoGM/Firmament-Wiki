- **Prerequisite:** [[Bard]]
- **Level:** 2
- **Tag:** #Vitality 
- **Type:** #FlexibleFeat 

- **Target:** 1 creature you can see
- **Range:** sight
- **Action Cost:** ---
- **Duration:** #Instantaneous 

**Fundamental:**
When you heal with [[Bard#Arcane Art|Arcane Art]], you can either heal an additional amount of [[Greater and Lesser Healing|Greater Healing]] to the target, or heal a second target.

- **Trained:** heal 1d8 [[Greater and Lesser Healing|Greater Healing]]
- **Expert:** [[Bard#Arcane Art|Arcane Art]] and Healers Song roll a d12 instead of a d8.
- **Master:** Once per scene, you can forgo rolling your performance check and get a guaranteed crit instead.
