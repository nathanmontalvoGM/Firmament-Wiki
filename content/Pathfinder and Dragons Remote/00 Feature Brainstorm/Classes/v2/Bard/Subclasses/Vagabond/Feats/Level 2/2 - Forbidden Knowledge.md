**Fundamental:**
During your travels you have delved into knowledge that most have been trying to keep secret. This knowledge has cursed you. You gain a new area of expertise for your Bardic Lore called *Forbidden Knowledge.* You can use this area of expertise to recall knowledge on any subject. On a success, the GM rolls a d20. On an 11 or higher you learn a truthful response. On a 10 or below you learn a false response.
