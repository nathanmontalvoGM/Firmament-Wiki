- **Prerequisite:** [[Bard]]
- **Level:** 2
- **Tag:** #emotion #PerformingArt 
- **Type:** #FlexibleFeat 

- **Targets:** a number of creatures equal to your charisma modifier that can see or hear you within range.
- **Range:** 30 foot aura
- **Action Cost:** 1
- **Duration:** 1 minute

**Fundamental:** 
Flavor Text. Allies that start or enter their turn within 30 feet and can see or hear your Courageous Performance gain the [[01 Condition#Inspired|Inspired]] condition.​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​ While [[01 Condition#Inspired|Inspired]], they gain [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]] on saving throws to be Frightened for the duration. They also gain a bonus to damage rolls determined by your training. 

- **Trained:** +2 to damage rolls.
- **Expert:** +4 to damage rolls and if an ally rolls critical fail against saving throw to be frightened, they gain a fail instead. 
- **Master:** +8 to damage rolls and an ally can’t be [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Hindered]] on saving throws to be Frightened.
