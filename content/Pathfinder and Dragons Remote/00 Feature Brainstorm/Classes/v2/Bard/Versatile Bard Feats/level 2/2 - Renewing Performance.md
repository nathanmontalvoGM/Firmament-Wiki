**Fundamental:**
Flavor Text. Allies that start or enter their turn within 30 feet and can see or hear your Regenerative Performance gain the [[01 Condition#Inspired|Inspired]] condition.​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​ While [[01 Condition#Inspired|Inspired]], the next time the target(s) receive healing, they gain an additional amount of healing determined by your training. The additional healing is the same type of healing ([[Greater and Lesser Healing|Greater Healing]] or [[Greater and Lesser Healing|Lesser Healing]]) as the initial healing received. After they receive the healing,c they are no longer inspired.

- **Trained:** You roll an additional die of healing.
- **Expert:** As trained, but the die value is a d12 for all healing dice rolled. 
- **Master:** As expert, but you treat all rolls of 3 or bellow as a 4.
