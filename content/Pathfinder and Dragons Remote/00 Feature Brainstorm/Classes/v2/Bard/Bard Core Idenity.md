# Core Mechanics
## What is the class’s signature resource or mechanic?
**Performing Arts.**
A bard can start a performance that lasts a minute and requires the bard to sustain the performance. Depending on the chosen performance (most likely determined by subclass) the Bards allies will gain temporary boons. 

**Bardic Lore.**
Allows bards to use their past experiences to glean knowledge and insight into creatures, locations or items. Bards can learn new areas of knowledge by using downtime activities and a Bardic Lore roll.

## What does this resource encourage players to do?
Rally around a bard and have him lead the battle
