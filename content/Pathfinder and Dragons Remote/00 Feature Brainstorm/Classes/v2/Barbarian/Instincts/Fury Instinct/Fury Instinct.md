## 1st Level - Instinct Ability
Your rage damage goes from 2 to 3. You roll an additional [[Weapon Dice|Weapon Die]] on your weapon attacks for every 3 stacks of Temper you have. 

## 1st Level - Breaking Point
* Immediately make an attack against the nearest target within melee range, rolling randomly if multiple valid targets exist.
* If there is no valid target within range, you must move up to half your movement speed till the nearest target, rolling randomly if multiple valid targets exist.
* If none of the criteria can be met, you take 1d6 X half your level (rounded down) psychic damage. This damage ignores all resistances and immunities.

## 7th - Specialization Ability

Your mind is filled with rage and nothing else. Your rage damage increased from 3 to 7. While raging any d20 roll for Intelligence, Wisdom or Charisma is rolled with  [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]]. 

## 9th - Raging Resistance 
Your mind is as resilient as your body. You resist psychic damage as well as [[physical damage]]. You also gain  [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]] on constitution checks to end your Rage.
