- **Level:** 4  
- **Tag:** #Vitality #Rage 
- **Type:** #RudimentaryFeat

- **Target:** Self  
- **Range:** —  
- **Action Cost:** —  
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

**Fundamental:**  
Your body rallies when pushed too far.

---

- **Trained:** Once per scene, when you would gain the [[01 Condition#Bloodied (WIP)|Bloodied]] condition, gain Temporary [[Hit Points]] equal to twice your level.
- **Expert:** Gain Temporary [[Hit Points]] equal to your twice your level + Constitution modifier.
- **Master:** You can repeat a save against a condition you have, or remove one if no save is associated with it.
- **Legendary:** You may instead trigger this when reduced to 0 HP but before falling [[01 Condition#Unconscious|Unconscious]].
