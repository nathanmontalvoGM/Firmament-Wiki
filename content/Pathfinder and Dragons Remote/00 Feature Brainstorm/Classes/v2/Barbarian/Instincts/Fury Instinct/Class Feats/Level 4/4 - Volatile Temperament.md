**Type:** #RudimentaryFeat 

**Target:** Self  
**Range:** —

**Fundamental:**  
Your rage builds with every blow.

When you hit with a melee attack while raging, gain bonuses based on your current Temper.

---

**Trained:** +1 damage per 3 Temper.  
**Expert:** +2 damage per 3 Temper.  
**Master:** +3 damage per 3 Temper.  
**Legendary:** +4 damage per 3 Temper.
