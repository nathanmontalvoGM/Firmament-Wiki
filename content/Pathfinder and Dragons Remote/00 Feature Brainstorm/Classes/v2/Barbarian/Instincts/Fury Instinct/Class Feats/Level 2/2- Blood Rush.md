- **Prerequisite:** [[Fury Instinct]]
- **Level:** 2
- **Tag:** #Haste #Rage 
- **Type:** #FlexibleFeat 



**Fundamental:**
While raging, if a creature within 30 feet has the [[01 Condition#Bloodied (WIP)|Bloodied]] condition, you gain bonus determined by your training. 

- **Trained:** You gain a 10 foot bonus to your movement speed
- **Expert:** the movement speed bonus increases to 20 feet, and you don't trigger [[Opportunity Attack]]s from creatures with the [[01 Condition#Bloodied (WIP)|Bloodied]] condition. 
- **Master:** You gain [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]] on your first attack this turn. This feat gains the #Strike tag in addition to the #Haste tag.
