**Prerequisite:** [[Fury Instinct]]
**Level:** 2
**Tag:** #Haste #Rage 
**Type:** #FlexibleFeat 

**Source:**

**Fundamental:**
When you enter a rage, you can move a number of feet as a free action determined by your training in this feat.

**Trained:** 10 feet
**Expert:** 20 feet, you ignore [[Strained Movement]] and you ignore [[Opportunity Attack]]s. 
**Master:** 30 feet you ignore [[Impeded Movement]] and you remove the [[01 Condition#Slowed|Slowed]] condition if it is affecting you.
**Legendary:** 40 feet gain [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]] on your next attack this turn. This feat gains the #Strike tag in addition to the #Haste tag.
