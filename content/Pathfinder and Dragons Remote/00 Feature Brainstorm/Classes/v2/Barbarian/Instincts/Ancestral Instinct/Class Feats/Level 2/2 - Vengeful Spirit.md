- **Prerequisite:** [[Barbarian]]. [[Ancestral Instinct]]
- **Level:** 2
- **Tag:** #Strike #Rage 
- **Type:** #FlexibleFeat 
- **Trigger:** You take damage while Raging.

- **Target:** target of your melee weapon attack
- **Range:** range of your melee weapon
- **Action Cost:** ---
- **Duration:** #Instantaneous 

**Fundamental:**
Your ancestors demand retribution. Your next melee attack before the end of your turn deals an additional force damage determined by your training. 

- **Trained:** 1d8 Force damage. If that attack hits a creature that damaged you this round, the damage increases to 2d8.
- **Expert:** As Trained. The base damage and secondary damage increases by 1d8. 
- **Master:** As Expert. The base damage and secondary damage increases by 1d8. If that attack hits a creature that damaged you this round, they must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or become [[01 Condition#Frightened|Frightened]]
