## Cantrips
```dataview
TABLE WITHOUT ID
  file.link AS "Spell",
  casting-time AS "Casting Time",
  duration AS "Duration",
  components AS "Components",
  casting_requirements AS "Casting Requirements"
FROM "00 Feature Brainstorm/Spell Casting/Rules/V2/Spells/Cantrips" AND #OccultTradition
SORT file.name ASC
```
