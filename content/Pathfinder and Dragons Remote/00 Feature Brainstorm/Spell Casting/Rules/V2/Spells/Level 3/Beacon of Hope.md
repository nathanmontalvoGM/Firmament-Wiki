**Spell Level:** 3
**Tradition:** #DivineTradition 
**Casting Time:** 3 actions
**Components:** #Vitality 
**Casting Requirements:** 
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute.

This spell bestows hope and vitality. For the duration, each target is [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]] on Wisdom saving throws, their d20 rolls aren't [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Hindered]] while [[01 Condition#Staggered|Staggered]], and they regain an additional 4 [[Greater and Lesser Healing|Greater Healing]] when [[Hit Points]] are restored.

**Empower (3):** increase the healing by 4 and you pick an additional ability to gain [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]].
