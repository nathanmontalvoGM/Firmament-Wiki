- **Spell Level:** 1
- **Tradition:** #DivineTradition 
- **Casting Time:** 2 actions
- **Components:** #Aegis
- **Casting Requirements:** #Verbal #Somatic 
- **Duration:** Concentration, 1 Minute

A divine shield is placed on the arms of your target. The target can use the [[Raise Shield]] action to instead raise this divine shield, gaining a +1 circumstance bonus to AC. It can [Shield Block](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=839) with its Raised arm as well; when it does, the target reduces the damage as if it had a shield with a Hardness of 1d4. 

**Empower:** Increase the AC bonus and the Hardness by 1.
