**Spell Level:** 1
**Tradition:** #Divination 
**Casting Time:** 2 actions
**Components:** #Aegis
**Casting Requirements:** #Verbal #Somatic 
- **Duration:** Concentration, 1 Minute

A divine shield is placed on the arms of your target. The target can use the [[Raise Shield]] action to instead raise this divine shield, gaining a +1 circumstance bonus to AC. It can [Shield Block](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=839) with its Raised arm as well; when it does, the target reduces the damage as if it had a shield with Hardness of 1d4. 

**Empower:** Increase the AC bonus by 1 and the hardness gain a +1 bonus to the damage blocked
