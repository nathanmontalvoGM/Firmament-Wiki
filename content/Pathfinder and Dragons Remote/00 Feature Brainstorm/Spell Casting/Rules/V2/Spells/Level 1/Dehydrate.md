- **Spell Level:** 1
- **Tradition:** #OccultTradition 
- **Casting Time:** 2 actions
- **Components:** 
- **Casting Requirements:** #Verbal #Somatic 
- **Duration:** 1 Round

Your enemies start to feel their throat parch and dry. Each target must succeed on a constitution saving throw or become [[01 Condition#Weakened (Strength)|Weakened]] for the duration. While [[01 Condition#Weakened (Strength)|Weakened]]  in this way, the target(s) gain a -2 penalty to attack rolls and spell DC to any spells cast with #Somatic components.

**Empower:** increase the duration of the spell by 1 Round and the penalty by 1.
