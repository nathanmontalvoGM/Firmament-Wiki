- **Spell Level:** cantrip
- **Tradition:** #ArcaneTradition #OccultTradition #PrimalTradition 
- **Casting Time:** 1 Reaction
- **Components:** #Fire #Evocation 
- **Casting Requirements:** #Verbal #Somatic 
- **Duration:** until the end of your next turn
- **Trigger** You would take fire damage.

You consume and ingest fire, making it less harmful to you. You gain resistance 5 to fire damage until the end of the current turn. During the remaining duration of the spell, you can use the Flame Belch action. The spell ends if you fall [[01 Condition#Unconscious|Unconscious]]  inhale, or exhale (this includes speaking).

*Delivery Exception:* this spells delivery is used on the Flame Belch action as opposed to the spells range.

**Flame Belch**
1 action. Use the spells Delivery to determine the range of Flame Belch. All creatures within range must make a basic dexterity saving throw or take 2d4 fire damage.

**Empower:** increase the damage by 1d4 and the fire resistance by 2.

**At Higher Levels:** This spell is automatically empowered by 1 level when you reach 5th level and once more at 9th level.
