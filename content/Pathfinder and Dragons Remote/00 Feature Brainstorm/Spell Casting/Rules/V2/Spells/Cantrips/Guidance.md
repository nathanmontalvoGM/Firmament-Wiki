- **Spell Level:** cantrip
- **Tradition:** #DivineTradition
- **Casting Time:** 1 action 
- **Components:** #Divination 
- **Casting Requirements:** #Verbal #Somatic 
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You call for divine blessing. Once before the spell ends, the target can roll a d4 and add the number rolled to any d20 roll of its choice. It can roll the die before or after making the ability check. The spell then ends and the creature is immune to its effects for 10 minutes.

**Empower (2):** the target rolls an additional d4 on the roll

**At Higher Levels:** This spell is automatically empowered by 1 level when you reach 5th level and once more at 9th level.
