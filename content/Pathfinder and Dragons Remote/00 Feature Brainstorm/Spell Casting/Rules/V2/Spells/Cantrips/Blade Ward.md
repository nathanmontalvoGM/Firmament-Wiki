- **Spell Level:** Cantrip
- **Tradition:** #ArcaneTradition #OccultTradition 
- **Casting Time:** 2 actions
- **Components:** #Aegis, #Physical, #Abjuration
- **Casting Requirements:** #Verbal #Somatic 
- **Duration:** 1 Round
- 
You extend your hand and trace a sigil of warding in the air. Until the end of your next turn, you have resistance 5 against bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage dealt by weapon attacks.

**Empower:** Increase the resistance by 2 and the duration by 1 round

**At Higher Levels:** This spell is automatically empowered by 1 level when you reach 5th level and once more at 9th level.
