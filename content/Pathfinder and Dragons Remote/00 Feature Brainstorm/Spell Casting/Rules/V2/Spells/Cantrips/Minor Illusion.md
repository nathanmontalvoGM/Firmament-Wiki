- **Spell Level:** Cantrip
- **Tradition:** #ArcaneTradition #OccultTradition
- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Components:** #Illusion
- **Casting Requirements:** #Somatic #Material
- **Duration:** 1 minute

You create either a sound or a silent image within range. The image is no larger than a 5-foot cube and the sound can range in volume from a whisper to a scream. Neither effect produces additional sensory output beyond sight and sound. The illusion can move with a movement speed of 10. A creature that uses an action to examine if an effect is an illusion with a successful investigation check against your spell save DC.

**Empower (2):** the duration extends by 10 minutes, the images size increases by 5 feet and the DC to identify illusions increase by 2.

**At Higher Levels:** This spell is automatically empowered by 1 level when you reach 5th level and once more at 9th level.
