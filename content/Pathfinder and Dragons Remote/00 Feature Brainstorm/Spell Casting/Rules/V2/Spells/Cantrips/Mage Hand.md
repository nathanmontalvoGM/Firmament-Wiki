- **Spell Level:** cantrip
- **Tradition:** #ArcaneTradition #OccultTradition 
- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Components:** #Conjuration
- **Casting Requirements:** #Verbal #Somatic 
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute. 
- 
You create a spectral floating hand at a point you can see within 30 feet. The hand acts as a spellcasting focus, allowing you to cast spells through it using its position to determine range and other parameters. You can move the hand up to 15 feet as part of casting a spell through it. The hand vanishes if it is ever more than 60 feet from you, if you cast this spell again, or if it takes 10 damage from any source.

**Delivery Exception:** When you choose a Delivery for this spell, it is applied to the Arcane Attack rather than when the spell is cast.

**Arcane Attack:** Free action once per turn, 1 Mana. You cast a cantrip through the hand using its position to determine range and delivery. 

**Empower (2):** increase the level of spell you can through mage hand by 1 and its hit points increase by 1 

**At Higher Levels:** This spell is automatically empowered by 1 level when you reach 5th level and once more at 9th level.
