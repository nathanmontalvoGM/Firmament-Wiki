- **Spell Level:** cantrip
- **Tradition:** #ArcaneTradition #DivineTradition 
- **Casting Time:** 2 actions
- **Components:** #necromancy 
- **Casting Requirements:** #Verbal #Somatic 
- **Duration:** #Instantaneous 

You point at one creature you can see within range, and the sound of a dolorous bell fills the air around it for a moment. The target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or take 1d8 void damage. If the target is [[01 Condition#Bloodied (WIP)|Bloodied]] it instead takes 1d12 void damage.

**Empower:** increase the base damage by 1d8 and the secondary damage by 1d12

**At Higher Levels:** This spell is automatically empowered by 1 level when you reach 5th level and once more at 9th level.
