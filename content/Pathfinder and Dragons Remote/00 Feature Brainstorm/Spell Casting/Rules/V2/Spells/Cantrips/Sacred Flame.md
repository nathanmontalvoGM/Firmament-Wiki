- **Spell Level:** cantrip
- **Tradition:** #DivineTradition 
- **Casting Time:** 2 actions
- **Components:** #Radiant #Evocation 
- **Casting Requirements:** #Verbal #Somatic 
- **Duration:** #Instantaneous


Flame-like radiance descends on a creature that you can see within range. The target must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or take 1d6 radiant damage. The target gains no benefit from cover for this saving throw. On a success the target is then illuminated in a soft glow until the start of your next turn, making it impossible for them to be [[01 Condition#Hidden|Hidden]]. 

**Empower:** increase damage by 1d6

**At Higher Levels:** This spell is automatically empowered by 1 level when you reach 5th level and once more at 9th level.
