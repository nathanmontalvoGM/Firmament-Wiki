**Spell Level:** 2
**Tradition:** #PrimalTradition 
**Casting Time:** 2 actions
**Components:** 
**Casting Requirements:** #Verbal #Somatic #Material 
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour.

A veil of shadows and silence radiates from you, masking you and your companions from detection. For the duration each target (including you) has a +2 bonus to Dexterity (Stealth) checks and can’t be tracked except by magical means. A creature that receives this bonus leaves behind no tracks or other traces of its passage.

**Empower (2):** increase the stealth bonus by +2
