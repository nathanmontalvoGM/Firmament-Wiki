**Spell Level:** 2
**Tradition:** #DivineTradition 
**Casting Time:** 2 actions
**Components:** 
**Casting Requirements:** 
- **Duration:** 1 Round

You force a creature to atone for their sins and transgressions. The target must make a wisdom saving throw. 

**Failure:** the target takes 1d6 psychic damage
**Critical Failure:** As failure, but they cannot use any [[Hostile Actions]] for the duration against a creature you choose as they are overwhelmed by their past guilt. 

**Empower:** the damage increases by 1d6
