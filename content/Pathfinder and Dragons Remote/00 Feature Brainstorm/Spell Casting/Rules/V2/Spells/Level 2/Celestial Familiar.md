**Spell Level:** 2
**Tradition:** #DivineTradition 
**Casting Time:** 3 actions
**Components:** #Summon 
**Casting Requirements:** #Verbal #Somatic #Material 
- **Duration:** 1 minute concentration

You summon a familiar to follow around a creature of your choosing for the duration. Regardless of the spells Delivery, this familiar is summoned within 5 feet of the caster. If the source targets a creature, it’s applied to the Sacrificial Heal feature Instead. Otherwise it’s cast with the spell as normal. The Delivery is instead applied to its Strike and Sacrificial Heal features. The familiar takes its turn during yours and has 3 actions. If the familiar must use a stat not listed in its stat block, the value is equal to the caster level. 

**Size:** Small
**Speed:** 25 feet
**[[Hit Points]]:** 8 times + 4 times the mana spent on cast
**AC:** 10 + 2 times the mana spent on cast
**Actions:**
* **Strike:** Delivery Range, 1 action, +6 + 2 times the mana spent on cast to hit, **Damage** 1d4 radiant
* **Sacrificial Heal:** Delivery Range, 2 action, The familiar restores a number of [[Hit Points]] equal to 5 times the mana spent on cast. This healing is [[Greater and Lesser Healing|Greater Healing]]. When it uses this feature, it expends its remaining energy and vanishes, ending the spell.

**Empower (2):** increase the Strike damage by 1d4 and you roll an additional 1d8 [[Greater and Lesser Healing|Greater Healing]] on Sacrificial Heal.
