**Spell Level:** 2
**Tradition:** 
**Casting Time:** 2 actions
**Components:** 
**Casting Requirements:** 
- **Duration:** 1 round

A blinding flash of light illuminates those around you. Any [[01 Condition#Invisible|Invisible]] creature are revealed until the start of your next turn. Targets must make a dexterity saving throw or be [[01 Condition#Dazzled|Dazzled]] by the light. 

**Empower (2):** Increase the DC of the saving throw by 2
