**Spell Level:** 2
**Tradition:** #DivineTradition 
**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Components:** 
**Casting Requirements:** 
- **Duration:** 1 hour 

You draw on knowledge from spirits of the past. Choose one skill. For the spell's duration, you have a +2 bonus to d20 rolls with that skill. The spell ends early if you cast it again.

**Empower:** increase the bonus by +1
