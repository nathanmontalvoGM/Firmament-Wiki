- **Spell Level:** 2
- **Tradition:** #DivineTradition 
- **Casting Time:** 2 actions
- **Components:** #Divination 
- **Casting Requirements:** #Verbal #Somatic 
- **Duration:** 1 Round

You help a creature with an afflicting condition. You grant a +2 bonus to the saving throw for a condition afflicting the target Until the end of their next turn. 

**Empower (3):** Pick a condition that has afflicted the creature within the last 24 hours. They are [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]] on d20 rolls to save against that condition until the end of their next turn.
