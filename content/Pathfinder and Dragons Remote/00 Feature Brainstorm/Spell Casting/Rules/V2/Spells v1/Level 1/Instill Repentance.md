**Tradition:** #DivineTradition 
**Casting Time:** 2 actions
- **Range:** 30 feet
**Targets:** 1 creature
**Components:** 
**Casting Requirements:** 
- **Duration:** 1 Round
**Stability:** 

You force a creature to atone for their sins and transgressions. The target must make a wisdom saving throw. On a failure they take 1d6 psychic damage and cannot use any [[Hostile Actions]] against a creature you choose as they are overwhelmed by their past guilt.
