- **Tradition:** #DivineTradition 
- **Casting Time:** 3 actions
- **Range:** 30 feet
**Targets:** 1 creature
- **Components:** 
- **Casting Requirements:** #Verbal #Somatic #Material 
- **Duration:** 1 minute concentration
**Stability:** #Stable 

You summon a familiar to follow around a creature of your choosing for the duration. This familiar takes its turn during your targets. If the familiar must use a stat not listed in its stat block, the value is equal to the caster level. 

**Size:** Small
**Speed:** 25 feet
**[[Hit Points]]:** 8 times spell level
**AC:** 10 + 2 times spell level
**Actions:**
* **Melee:** 1 action, +6 + 2 times spell level, **Damage** 1d8 radiant + 1d8 per spell level above 1st ( to a maximum of 5d8)
* **Ranged:** 30 feet, 1 action, +6 + 2 times spell level, **Damage** 1d4 radiant + 1d4 per spell level above 1st ( to a maximum of 5d4)
* **Sacrificial Heal:** Touch, 2 action, The familiar restores a number of [[Hit Points]] equal to 5 times the spells level. This healing is [[Greater and Lesser Healing|Greater Healing]]. When it uses this feature, it vanishes and the spell ends.
