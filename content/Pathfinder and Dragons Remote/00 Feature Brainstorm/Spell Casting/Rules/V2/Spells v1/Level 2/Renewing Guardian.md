**Tradition:** #DivineTradition 
**Casting Time:** 3 actions
- **Range:** 60 feet
**Targets:** up to 3 creatures
**Components:** 
**Casting Requirements:** #Verbal #Somatic #Material 
- **Duration:** 1 minute
**Stability:** #Stable 

You summon a divine guardian to provide healing to your allies. The renewing guardian is a divine being that is a medium size incorporeal creature that has no [[Hit Points]] or movement. When you cast this spell, choose a source to apply to the spell as normal. If the source targets a creature, Instead of source being cast with the spell, it is instead imbued with the guardian. An ally can use an [[interact]] action on the guardian to be healed 2d10 [[Greater and Lesser Healing|Greater Healing]] and gain the benefits of the applied source. Once they use this effect, they are immune to the spell for 24 hours. If the source does not target a creature, the source is not imbued with the guardian and the spell is cast as normal.

If an enemy attempts to willingly interact with the guardian they must make a wisdom saving throw. On a failure, they take 2d10 radiant damage.

The spell expires when 3 creatures are healed by it or its duration ends, whichever happens first.

**At Higher Levels:** The healing and damage increase by 1d10.
