**Casting Requirements:**

You draw on knowledge from spirits of the past. Choose one skill in which you are untrained. For the spell's duration, you have expert proficiency in the chosen skill. The spell ends early if you cast it again.

**At higher Levels** (5th) The proficiency rank of the chosen skill increases to master and you can select two skills. (8th) The proficiency rank of the chosen skill increases to legendary and you can select three skills.
