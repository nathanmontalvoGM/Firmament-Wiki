**Tradition:** #ArcaneTradition #PrimalTradition 
**Casting Time:** 2 actions
**Range:** 30 feet
**Targets:** 1 creature
**Components:** #Physical #Electricity 
**Casting Requirements:** #Verbal #Somatic #Material
**Duration:** #Instantaneous 
**Stability:** #Unstable

You conjure up a length of sharp copper filament humming with electrical current that strikes out at your foe. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, the wire deals 1d6 slashing damage and 1d6 electricity damage.

**Critical Success:** The target takes double damage and 1d4 persistent electricity damage.
**Failure:** The target takes the electricity damage, but not the slashing damage.  
**Critical Failure** The target is unaffected. Roll slashing and electricity damage. You take the higher of the two is dealt to you instead, applying resistances and immunities as normal.

**At Higher Levels:** The slashing damage, initial electricity damage, and persistent electricity damage on a critical hit each increase by 1d6 when you reach 5th level (2d6), 11th level (3d6), and 17th level (4d6).
