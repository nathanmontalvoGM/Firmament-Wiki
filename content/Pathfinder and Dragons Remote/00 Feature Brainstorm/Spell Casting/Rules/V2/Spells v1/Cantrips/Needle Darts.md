**Tradition:** #ArcaneTradition #DivineTradition #OccultTradition #PrimalTradition 
**Casting Time:** 2 actions
**Range:** 60 Feet.
**Targets:** 1 creature
**Components:** #Physical 
**Casting Requirements:** #Somatic #Material 
**Duration:** #Instantaneous 
**Stability:** #Volatile 

You shape three needles out of a piece of metal in your possession and send them flying in a tight group toward one target. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, the targets takes 3d4 piercing damage. All of the needles impart any special properties of the metal that forms them; for instance, [cold iron](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=272) needles deal additional damage to creatures with weakness to cold iron.

**Critical Success:** The target takes double damage and 1 persistent bleed damage
**Critical Failure:** The target is unaffected. You rush the needless shape, pricking your own hands in the process. You take 1 persistent bleed damage. 

**At Higher Levels:** You send one additional needle, increasing the regular damage by 1d4 and increasing the persistent bleed damage on a critical hit and critical failure by 1 when you reach 5th level (4d4 and 2), 11th level (5d4 and 3), and 17th level (6d4 and 4).
