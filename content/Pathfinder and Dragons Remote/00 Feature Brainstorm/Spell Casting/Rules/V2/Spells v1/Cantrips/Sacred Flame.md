- **Tradition:** #DivineTradition 
- **Casting Time:** 2 actions
- **Range:** 60 feet
**Targets:** 1 creature
- **Components:** #Radiant #Evocation 
- **Casting Requirements:** #Verbal #Somatic 
- **Duration:** #Instantaneous
**Stability:** #Stable 

Flame-like radiance descends on a creature that you can see within range. The target must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or take 1d8 radiant damage. The target gains no benefit from cover for this saving throw. On a success the target is then illuminated in a soft glow until the start of your next turn, making it impossible for them to be [[01 Condition#Hidden|Hidden]]. 

**At Higher Levels:** The spell's damage increases by 1d8 when you reach 5th level (2d8), 11th level (3d8), and 17th level (4d8).
