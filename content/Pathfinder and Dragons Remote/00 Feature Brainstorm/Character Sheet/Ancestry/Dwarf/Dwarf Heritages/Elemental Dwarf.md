A part of you resides in the elemental planes. During your [[daily preparations]] you can tap into part of your elemental heritage. Choose one of the following damage types: acid, cold, electricity, or fire. You gain the Elemental Emanation action. 

## At higher Levels
### 1st Elemental Emanation
2 actions
Frequency: once per hour
Energy bursts forth from your body. Make a weapon attack. Any creature hit with this attack must make a Basic [[Dexterity]] saving throw or take 1d6 damage of your chosen type. This damage increase by 1d6 at 3rd level, and every 2 levels after.
