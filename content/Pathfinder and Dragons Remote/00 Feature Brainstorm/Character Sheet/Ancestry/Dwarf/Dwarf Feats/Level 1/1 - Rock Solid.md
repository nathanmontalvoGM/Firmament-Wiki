- **Prerequisite:** [[Mountain Dwarf]]
- **Level:** 1
- **Tag:** #Steadfast

Your innate connection to stone makes you adept at moving across uneven surfaces. You can ignore difficult terrain caused by stone (such as rubble or gravel) and uneven ground made of stone and earth. Additionally when you roll a success against an effect to reposition you (such as a [[shove]] or [[trip]], but other effects can apply as well per GM discretion) you gain a Critical success instead.
