## 1st Elven Magic
Seer elves are defined by their close connection with the [[Firmament]]. Choose a magical tradition (Arcane, Primal, Divine or Occult). This effects features at higher levels. You learn two spells from that traditions spell list. You gain additional mana equal to 2 times half your level (rounded down). This mana can only be spent on the two spells you learned from Elven Magic. 

## 1st - Skill Proficiency

Seer elf’s gains training in the a skill determined by their tradition.
**Arcane:** You gain training in Arcana
**Primal:** You gain training in Nature
**Divine:** You gain training in Religion
**Occult:** You gain training in Occultism

## 5th - Something Magical

**Arcane:** You can cast [[Detect Magic]] and it’s always empowered by 1 level with no [[Mana]] cost. 
**Primal:** You can speak with animals and plants at will as if you had the Spell [[Speak with Animals]] and [[Speak with Plants]] cast on you. 
**Divine:** You can cast [[00 Feature Brainstorm/Spell Casting/Rules/V2/Spells/Level 1/Heal|Heal]] and its always empowered by 1 level with no [[Mana]] cost. 
**Occult:** You can cast SOMETHING
