## 1st Skillful Artisan 
The type of creature you harvest from affects the properties of items crafted with your Cunning Artisan feature. You only gain these benefits while holding, wearing, or raising the crafted item. You cannot benefit from two crafted items of the same type simultaneously.
- ### **Beast.** This item is well balanced and light weight. 
	- **Trained.** Weapons crafted with this material gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls. Armor crafted with this material gains a +2 bonus to stealth checks and shields gain a +1 bonus to AC. 
	- **Expert.** Increase the attack roll bonus to +2, the armors stealth bonus to +4 and the shields AC bonus to +2.
	- **Master.** Increase the attack roll bonus to +3, the armors stealth bonus to +6 and the shields AC bonus to +3.
- ### **Construct.** This item is robust and sturdy. 
	- **Trained:** Weapons crafted with this material deals an extra [[Weapon Dice|Weapon Die]] of damage on [[Critical Hits|Critical Hit]]. Armor crafted with this material reduces your damage from [[Critical Hits|Critical Hits]] by -2 and the hardness for shields increases by 1d6.
	- **Expert:** Weapons crafted with this material deals +2 damage. Armor's [[Critical Hits|Critical Hit]] reduction increases to 4. Shield hardness increases by a further 1d6.
	- **Master:** Weapons crafted with this material deals +4 damage and [[01 Condition#Dazzled|Dazzle]] targets on a [[Critical Hits|Critical Hit]]. Armor's [[Critical Hits|Critical Hit]] reduction increases to 6 and the hardness for shields increases by a further 1d6.
- ### **Dragon.** This item carries the remains of the dragons essence. 
	- **Trained:** Weapons change their damage type to match the harvested dragon's breath weapon. Armor grants resistance 3 to that damage type. Shields grants resistance 3 to that damage type when using [[Shield Block]] and you can use [[Shield Block]] against effects that would deal the same damage type. 
	- **Expert:** Weapons deal an additional 1 persistent damage of that damage type. Armor and shield resistance increases to 5. 
	- **Master:** Weapons deal an additional 1d4 persistent damage of that damage type. On a [[Critical Hits|Critical Hit]], you can choose to have all creatures within 5 feet make a [[Basic Saving Throw|Basic]] dexterity saving throw or take 2d6 of that damage type. Armor and resistance increases to 8 and the resistance doubles while [[01 Condition#Bloodied (WIP)|Bloodied]]. 
- ### **Monstrosity:** The item is unsettling to behold. 
	- **Trained:** You gain a +2 bonus to intimidation checks if you use this item as part of the check. Additionally, once per [[Long Rest]] you can force 1 creature within 30 feet that you can see to make a wisdom saving throw. On a failure, they are [[01 Condition#Frightened|Frightened]] for 1 round.
	- **Expert:** The Intimidation bonus increases to +4. The [[01 Condition#Frightened|Frightened]] effect can now target up to 2 creatures simultaneously. The effect now lasts 1 minute but affected creatures can repeat the save at the end of each of their turns. 
	- **Master:** The Intimidation bonus increases to +6 and you gain [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]] on all Intimidation checks made in combat. The Frightened effect can now target up to 3 creatures.
- ### **Plant Creature.** The item is covered in fine thorns.  
	- **Trained:** Weapons crafted from this material deal an additional 1d4 persistent bleed damage on a [[Critical Hits|Critical Hit]]. Creatures that critically fail a touch attack against a target wearing armor or raising a shield crafted from this material take 1d4 persistent bleed damage in return.
	- **Expert:** Weapons now also deal 1d4 persistent bleed damage on hit and the bleed damage on crit is doubled. Armor and shield retaliation damage increases to 2d4 and also triggers on a regular failure rather than only a critical failure.
	- **Master:** Weapon bleed damage increases to 2d4. Armor and shield retaliation increases to 3d4. 
## 5th - Versatile Artisan
You can craft more complex items with your cunning artisan feature. In addition to the items granted by your cunning artisan feature, add the following to your list of craftable items.
- When you gain this feat, choose 3 weapons from the melee [[martial weapons]] list. You may add one additional martial weapon to your list at each level thereafter.

## 9th - Masterful Artisan
You've mastered the craft of being a cunning artisan.
- You're weapons, armor and shields count as magical.
- You can craft any simple item under 200gp (GM discretion). These items take a full day to make as opposed to 1 hour.
- You can now choose one item to be your **Magnum Opus.** If you have the materials from two different creatures, you can combine them into one item. You can only have one Magnum Opus made.
