## 1st Skillful Artisan 
The items you craft with your Cunning Artisan feature are more robust and well-made. 
### Armor (stats of [[Hide Armor]])
- **Trained:** Increase the AC bonus of armors you create by +1
- **Expert:** The AC bonus increases by +2
- **Master:** The AC bonus increases by +3
### Shield (stats of [[Light Armor]])
- **Trained:** The shields hardness increases by +2
- **Expert:** The shields hardness increases by +4
- **Master:** The shields hardness increases by +6
### Weapon (stats of a [[simple weapons|simple weapon]] of your choice)
- **Trained:** The attack and damage bonus of the weapon increases by +1
- **Expert:** The attack and damage bonus of the weapon increases by +2
- **Master:** The attack and damage bonus of the weapon increases by +3

The type of creature you harvest from also affects the properties of items crafted with your Cunning Artisan feature. You only gain these benefits while holding, wearing, or raising the crafted item. You cannot benefit from two crafted items of the same type simultaneously.

- **Beast.** This item is well balanced and light weight. Weapons crafted with this material gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls. Armor crafted with this material gains a +2 bonus to stealth checks and shields gain a +1 bonus to AC. 
- **Construct.** This item is robust and sturdy. Weapons crafted with this material deals an extra [[Weapon Dice|Weapon Die]] of damage on [[01 Critical Hits|Critical Hit]]. Armor crafted with this material reduces your damage from [[01 Critical Hits|Critical Hits]] by -2 and the hardness for shields increases by 1d6.
- **Dragon.** This item carries the remains of the dragons essence. Weapons change their damage type to match the harvested dragon's breath weapon. Armor grants resistance 3 to that damage type. Shields grants resistance 3 to that damage type when using [[Shield Block]] and you can use [[Shield Block]] against effects that would deal the same damage type. 
- **Monstrosity.** The item is unsettling to behold. You gain a +2 bonus to intimidation checks if you use this item as part of the check. Additionally, once per [[Long Rest]] you can force 1 creature within 30 feet that you can see to make a wisdom saving throw. On a failure, they are [[01 Condition#Frightened|Frightened]] for 1 round.
- **Plant Creature.** Weapons crafted from this material deal an additional 1d4 persistent bleed damage on a [[01 Critical Hits|Critical Hit]]. Creatures that critically fail a touch attack against a target wearing armor or raising a shield crafted from this material take 1d4 persistent bleed damage in return.


## 5th - Versatile Artisan
You can craft more complex items with your cunning artisan feature. In addition to the items granted by your cunning artisan feature, add the following to your list of craftable items.
- When you gain this feat, choose 3 weapons from the melee [[martial weapons]] list. You may add one additional martial weapon to your list at each level thereafter.
- A light crossbow and longbow. 
- Medium armor (with the stats of [[Hide Armor|Hide Armor]])

## 9th - Masterful Artisan
You've mastered the craft of being a cunning artisan.
- You're weapons, armor and shields count as magical.
- You can craft any simple item under 200gp (GM discretion). These items take a full day to make as opposed to 1 hour.
- You can now choose one item to be your **Magnum Opus.** If you have the materials from two different creatures, you can combine them into one item. You can only have one Magnum Opus made.
