> LOREEEE
 
 **[[Hit Points]]:** 8
* **Age:**
* **Speed:** 30
* **Ability Score Increase:** +1 to three stats, -1 to one stat
* **Features:** 
	* **Bite:** You have a fanged maw that you can use to make unarmed strikes. When you hit with it, the strike deals 1d6 + your Strength modifier slashing damage. On hit, if you deal damage, you heal half the damage dealt as [[Greater and Lesser Healing|Lesser Healing]]. 
	* **Natural Armor.** Your calculation for [[Unarmored Defense]] is 14 + level + training + dexterity instead of the normal calculation. 
	* **Breath Control.** You can hold your breath for 30 minutes. Additionally, you gain resistance equal to your level to inhaled threats such as poisons. 
	* **Cunning Artisan.** You can spend 1 hour to harvest bone and hide from a slain beast, construct, dragon, monstrosity, or plant creature of size Small or larger to create one of the following items: a [[Hide Shield]] , a light set of armor (with the same stats as [[leather armor]]) or a [[simple weapons|simple weapon]] of your choice. These items have little monetary value to anyone other than fellow Lizardfolk. To use this trait, you need a blade, such as a dagger, or appropriate artisan's tools, such as leatherworker's tools.
