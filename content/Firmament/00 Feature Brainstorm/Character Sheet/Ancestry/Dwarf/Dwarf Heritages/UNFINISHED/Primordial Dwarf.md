You have deep ties to your ancestors and can call on them for aid in times of crisis. You gain the Call on Ancestors feat.

## Call on Ancestors 
**Trigger:** You fail a d20 roll.
**Frequency:** Once per hour

A spirit from your past manifests to aid you in this task. You gain [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]] on the roll as if the [[00 Feature Brainstorm/Rules/subs systems/Subsystem Backlog/Combat/Actions/Aid]] action was used.
