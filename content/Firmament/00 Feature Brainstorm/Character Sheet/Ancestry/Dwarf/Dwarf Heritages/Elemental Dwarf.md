A part of you resides in the elemental planes. During your [[daily preparations]] you can tap into part of your elemental heritage. Choose one of the following damage types: acid, cold, electricity, or fire. Your Elemental Emanation feature gains this damage type.

## At higher Levels
### 1st Elemental Emanation
2 actions
**Frequency:** once per hour
Energy bursts forth from your body. Make a weapon attack. Any creature hit with this attack must make a Basic [[Dexterity]] saving throw or take xd6 damage of your chosen type. This damage increase as you gain training in this feat.

- **Trained:** 2d6
- **Expert:** 3d6
- **Master:** 4d6

### 5th - Resist the Elements
Flavor text. During your [[daily preparations]], you gain resistance equal to your level to your chosen damage type. 

### 9th - Elemental Command WIP
2 actions
**Frequency:** once per hour

You may create one of the following effects within 60 feet using your chosen damage type:
- **Wall:** Create a 10 long by 10 foot tall wall of your element that lasts for 1 minute. Creatures that pass through it take 3d6 damage of your chosen type. 
- **Burst:** Detonate a 15-foot burst centered on a point you can see. All creatures in the area make a Basic Dexterity saving throw or take 2d6 persistent damage of your chosen type. This effect than lingers in the area for 1 minute.
