You're ancestors lived among the stones and mountains, granting you a hearty resilience that makes you solid as a rock. 

## Stand Your Ground
At 1st level, Any effect that attempts to reposition you against your will (such as a [[shove]] or [[Trip]], but other effects can apply as well per GM discretion) is [[Hindered]]. 

## Mountain Life
Also at 1st level, your time in the mountains has given you an uncanny knowledge of their surroundings as wells as resilience in their climate. You gain [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]] on survival checks to traverse mountains terrain and you gain cold resistance equal to your level.

## Dwarven Durability
At 5th level, your healing surges are better. [[Healing Surge]] only costs 1 action and you can use [[Healing Surge]] an additional amount of times before you are immune to its effects determined by your training.

- **Trained:** You can use [[Healing Surge]] 2 times. You regain additional [[Hit Points]] equal to your level.
- **Expert:** You roll an additional [[Hit Dice]] on [[Healing Surge]] without expending one. 
- **Master:** You can use [[Healing Surge]] 3 times. When you use [[Healing Surge]], you can repeat a saving throw against a [[01 Condition]] afflicting you. 

## Become Stone
**Frequency** once per day  
**Trigger** An enemy or hazard’s effect hits you or you fail a constitution save against one.
1 reaction

The strength of stone overcomes you so strongly that it replaces your stout body. You become [[01 Condition#Petrified|Petrified]] until the start of your next turn. You don't take any damage from the triggering effect or any other ill effects that would afflict you.
