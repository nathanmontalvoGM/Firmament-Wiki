
## 1st 
**Frequency:** Once per [[Long Rest]]
**Trigger:** your [[Hit Points]] are reduced to 0

You've shrugged off death more times than you can count. When you drop to 0 [[Hit Points]] fill 1 segment of your [[Death Clock]] instead of 2.

## 5th - A Dwarf Never Faulters
**Frequency:** Once per day
**Trigger:** you would fall to 0 [[Hit Points]]

It would be a dishonor to your family if you fell so easily. Instead of dropping to 0 [[Hit Points]], you gain [[Temporary Hit Points]] equal to twice your level and fall to 1 [[Hit Points]] instead. 

## 9th
When you gain the [[01 Condition#Staggered|Staggered]] condition, your d20 rolls aren't [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Hindered]].
