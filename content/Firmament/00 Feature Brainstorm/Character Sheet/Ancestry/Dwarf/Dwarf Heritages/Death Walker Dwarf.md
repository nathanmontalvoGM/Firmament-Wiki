
## Injury Resistant 
At 1st level, you've shrugged off death more times than you can count. When you gain the [[01 Condition#Staggered|Staggered]] condition and roll for a [[Lingering Injury|Minor Injury]], roll 4d10 and pick and pick the highest two numbers for the table result. Additionally, damage can't incur your death clock.
 
## A Dwarf Never Faulters
At 5th level, When an effect would deal extra damage to you because you have the [[01 Condition#Bloodied (WIP)|Bloodied]] condition, you ignore the additional damage. Additionally, when you gain the [[01 Condition#Staggered|Staggered]] condition, you retain all your actions and your d20 rolls aren't [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Hindered]].

## Final Stand
It would be a dishonor to your family if you fell so easily. When you are reduced to 0 [[Hit Points]], you drop to 1 [[Hit Points|Hit Point]] instead and you gain [[Temporary Hit Points]] equal to twice your level.
