## 1st
Your blood is thick and tough and won't leave your body without a fight. You gain resistance to bleed damage equal to half your level and the DC of checks to remove [[01 Condition#Persistent Damage WIP|Persistent Damage]] from bleed effects is 5 instead of 15. 

## 5th
Flavor Text. You gain resistance equal to your level to additional damage caused by creatures exploiting the [[01 Condition#Bloodied (WIP)|Bloodied]] condition on you. 

## 9th
**Trigger:** You roll initiative
**Frequency:** once per hour
Flavor Text.  You gain temporary [[Hit Points]] equal to twice your level.
