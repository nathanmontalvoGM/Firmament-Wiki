# 1st - Jaws
You gain a bite attack as an unarmed attack. Your bite deals 1d6 piercing damage and counts as a weapon you are always considered proficient with, even if disarmed or grappled. This damage increases as you gain training in this feat. If you roll an 6 on the attack, you heal 2 [[Hit Points]] as [[Greater and Lesser Healing|Greater Healing]]. 

- **Trained:** as written
- **Expert:** 2d8. You heal 4 [[Hit Points]] and you heal on a 7 or 8.
- **Master:** 3d8. you heal 8 [[Hit Points]] and you heal on a 6, 7 or 8.

# 5th - Blood Frenzy
When you bite a creature and deal damage, you can choose to enter a blood frenzy. Until the end of your next turn you gain a bonus to melee attack and damage rolls determined by your training but are [[01 Condition#Off-Guard|Off-Guard]] until the start of your next turn.

- **Trained:** +3
- **Expert:**  +5
- **Master:** +8

# 9th - Feral 

While you are [[Bloodied]], your bite attack gains the following benefits: it deals an additional 1d8 persistent bleed damage, you cannot be [[01 Condition#Frightened|Frightened]], and creatures that take bleed damage from this effect are [[01 Condition#Weakened (Strength)|Weakened]].
