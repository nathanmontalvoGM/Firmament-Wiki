## 1st - Calculated Risk

Once per long rest before making a d20 roll you may declare you are taking a Calculated Risk. If the roll fails, you gain [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]] on your next attempt at the same or a similar roll. If it succeeds, you can reroll one d20 roll before your next [[Long Rest]].

- **Trained:** as written
- **Expert:** you can use this feature two times per [[Long Rest]]
- **Master:** you can use this feature three times per [[Long Rest]]

## 5th - Driven to Excel
Once per long rest when you succeed on a skill check, you may immediately attempt a harder version of the same task. The DC for the for the skill check you are attempting increases by 5. If you succeed on the second check, you gain a permeant +2 bonus to that skill. Once you've gained a bonus to a skill this way, you cannot benefit from this feature with that skill again. 
## 9th - Rise to the Occasion
Choose two skills you are trained in. You gain [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]] on all d20 rolls made with that skill. Additionally, you increase the degree of success on your [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]] die for these rolls on a 5 and 6.
