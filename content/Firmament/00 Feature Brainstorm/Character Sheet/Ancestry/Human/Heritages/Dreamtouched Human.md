> Your connection to the [[Firmament]] allows you to manipulate your dreams.

## 1st - Lucid Dreaming
You've learned to influence your dreams and gain benefits from them. Each night when you sleep, you may shape the content of your dream by choosing one of the following before you wake. You can only choose one per long rest and you lose the benefits on your next [[Long Rest]].
- ### **Dream of Battle.** 
	- **Trained.** You gain a +2 bonus to initiative rolls.
	- **Expert.** You gain a +4 bonus to initiative rolls.
	- **Master.** You gain a +5 bonus to initiative rolls. If you go first in initiative, your first d20 roll is [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]].
- ### **Dream of Knowledge.**  
	- **Trained.** Choose a skill you are untrained in. You gain a +2 bonus to d20 rolls with that skill
	- **Expert.** You can choose skills you have training in. Increase the bonus to +3.
	- **Master.** Select two skills instead of 1. Increase the bonus to +4.
- ### **Dream of Peace.** 
	- **Trained.** Gain 4 [[Temporary Hit Points]] 
	- **Expert.** Gain 8 [[Temporary Hit Points]]
	- **Master.** Gain 12 [[Temporary Hit Points]]

## 9th - Unbound Mind
You can pull aspects from your dreams into reality. When you gain this feat at 9th level, select either **Ethereal Magic** or **Uncanny Strength** and gain their benefits. 

- ### **Ethereal Magic.** 
	- You gain 4 additional Mana. Additionally, you can spend 2 mana to alter a spell you cast with one of the following effects:
	- **Shifted Figment:** Change the spell's damage type to one of the following: force, psychic, or radiant and increase its damage by one damage dice.
	- **Lingering Daydream:** If the spell has a duration, increase the spell's duration by 1 round.
	- **Vivid Hallucinations:** If the spell would require a saving throw, one target of your choice that fails their save also becomes [[01 Condition#Dazzled|Dazzled]] until the end of their next turn.
- You may only apply one alteration per casting.

- ### **Uncanny Strength.** 
	- You briefly channel the impossible feats of the dreamscape through your body. A number of times per long rest equal to your Constitution modifier (minimum 1), you may activate Uncanny Strength as a free action before making a Strength-based check, saving throw, or attack roll. Choose one of the following effects:
	- **Phantasmal Might:** Gain a +4 bonus to the triggering roll.
	- **Projected Reach:** Your melee attacks gains 5 feet of reach until the end of your turn.
	- **Manifested Fantasia:** If the triggering roll is a weapon attack and it hits, it deals force damage instead and deals an additional 1d8 force damage.
	- **Weightless Step:** Immediately move up to 10 feet without provoking [[Opportunity Attack]]s as part of the triggering action.
