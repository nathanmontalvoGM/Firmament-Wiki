## 1st - Well Travelled 
Flavor Text.
- **Trained:** Increase your base movement speed by 5 feet. Additionally, choose a favored terrain when tyou select this heritage: arctic, coast, desert, forest, grassland, or mountain. You can ignore [[Strained Movement]] in hits terrain. 
- **Expert:** Increase your base movement speed by an additional 5 feet. You can now ignore [[Impeded Movement]] in your chosen terrain.
- **Master:** Increase your base movement speed by an additional 5 feet. Chose a second terrain to be your favored terrain.

## 5th - Safe Haven
You and your party can rest in areas that would normally be uncomfortable. 
- **Trained:** You and your party gain the full benefits of a [[Long Rest]] even in areas without any shelter. Additionally, you and your party regain an additional 10 [[Hit Points]] when resting in your chosen terrain.
- **Expert:** Increase the additional [[Hit Points]] gained by 5. Additionally, you and your party wakes well rested and inspired. You and your party gains 1 [[Hero Points|Hero Point]].
- **Master:** Increase the additional [[Hit Points]] gained by 10. Additionally, secondary effect here.
