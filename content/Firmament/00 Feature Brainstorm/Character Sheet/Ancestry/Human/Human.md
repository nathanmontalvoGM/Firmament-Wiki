> Humans are often regarded as a young, impulsive and short-lived species with reckless ambition and adaptability. Their drive and passion has led them to be the worlds predominant ancestry. There is one thing however that humans alone possess that even the oldest elf cannot claim: dreams. Dreams are the closet any ancestry has been to the veil of the firmament. What this means, exactly, no one has fully agreed on.

 * **[[Hit Points]]:** 8
* **Age:**
* **Speed:** 30ft.
* **Ability Score Increase:** +1 to three stats
* **Features:**
	* **Adaptable Skill.** You are trained in a skill of your choice. When you level up, you can choose to gain training in a different skill. 
	* **Feat.** You gain a [[General Feat]] as a bonus feat.
	* **Restful Dreams.** When you complete a long rest, you may recall one detail from a dream you had. Once per long rest you may ask the GM a yes-or-no question about something that has happened to you within the last 7 days. The GM will answer truthfully.
