> Woodland elves are shaped by the environment they grow up in, becoming reshaped by it. 
## 1st Elven Magic WIP

## 1st 
You gain a climb speed equal to your movement speed. This climb speed can only be used for climbing trees, vines or other foliage. You also ignore [[Strained Movement]] when in forests.

## 5th Elven Woodworking
One per [[Long Rest]], you spend 1 minute using twigs, logs or other bits of wood to reshape and form an object or item. The object can be no larger than 10 cubic feet. The objects total [[Hit Points]] is 20 and its BT is 10.
