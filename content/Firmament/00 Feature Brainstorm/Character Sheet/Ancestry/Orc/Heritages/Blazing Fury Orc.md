Your bloodline carries a fragment of the [[Firmament]]t's fire in their emotions. 
## 1st - Igniting Rage
Your anger burns hot and volatile, tapping into the planes of fire. You gain training in either Intimidation or Athletics. You gain fire resistance equal to half your level (rounded down). Additionally, when you use your **Intimidating Presence** feature, in addition to being [[01 Condition#Frightened|Frightened]] on a success, the target also takes persistent fire damage determined by your training. 
- **Trained:** 1d4 persistent fire damage
- **Expert:**  1d6 persistent fire damage. This damage ignores fire resistance.
- **Master:**  2d6 persistent fire damage This damage ignores fire immunity.

## 5th - Heat Haze
The air around you warps with thermal distortion, making you difficult to look at directly. Once per [[Long Rest]] you can spend two actions to gain one of the following effects for 1 minute. 

- Ranged attacks made against you from more than 30 feet away have [[Hindered]].
- Creatures that start their turn within 10 feet of you take 1d4 fire damage.
- When a creature fails a saving throw against your **Intimidating Presence**, you may cause them to be [[Dazzled]] as being [[Frightened]]. They lose the [[01 Condition#Dazzled|Dazzled]] condition when they lose the [[01 Condition#Frightened|Frightened]] condition.

## 9th - Conflagration
You intensify your rage to a searing level. Once per [[Long Rest]], you can spend two actions to channel your **Intimidating Presence** for 1 minute without needing to hit with an attack prior. While channeled in this way, all creatures that start their turn within 15 feet of you and that can see you must make a wisdom saving throw vs your [[Class DC]] or become [[01 Condition#Frightened|Frightened]]. They will remain [[01 Condition#Frightened|Frightened]] on subsequent turns as long as they start their turn within 15 of you. Otherwise, they can repeat the wisdom saving throw at the end of their turn to the end the effect. They also take the persistent fire damage from **Igniting Rage** as normal.
