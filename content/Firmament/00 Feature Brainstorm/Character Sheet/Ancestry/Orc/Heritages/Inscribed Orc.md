Your community holds special reverence for the markings on ones body. 

## 1st - Sturdy Skin
Your starting HP is 14 instead of 10.
## 1st - Tribal Tattoo
When you select this heritage at 1st level, you choose a tattoo from the list below to ink on your body. The location can be anywhere of your choosing and the tattoo can be used regardless of location. Each tattoo has a **passive** and **active** feature listed in its description. The passive effect is always present while the tattoo is inscribed while the active effect can be used a number of times equal to your training bonus in your [[Class DC]].
### Bone Tattoo
This tattoo resembles the skull or bones of a creature important to your tribe. 
- **Passive.** You gain resistance to necrotic damage equal to half your level (rounded down)
- **Active.** For 30 minutes mindless undead creatures react to you as if you were undead and regard you neutrally. Smarter undead require a Deception check to convince. You gain [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]] on rolls to do so.

### Blessed Tattoo
This tattoo resembles a deity or divine figure of importance to your tribe. 
- **Passive.** You gain resistance to radiant damage equal to half your level (rounded down).
- **Active.** When you are hit by an attack, you can use you reaction to ward yourself from that creature. For 1 minute, the next attack roll from that creature rolled against you gains two stacks of [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Hindered]]. 
### Glyph
This tattoo resembles glyphs in a language you don't understand. 
- **Passive.** You can use the glyph tattoo on your body as a spell casting focus.
- **Active.** You can pick a spell from any spell list and cast that spell as if you had prepared it. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for these spells when you cast them with this trait (choose when you select this ancestry). It uses [[Mana]] and spell slots as normal. 
## 5th - Tribal Mark
You're path as an adventurer has granted you a mark on your body as a symbol of your journey.
### Mark of Faith
- **Passive.** You receive an additional die of healing from magical healing.
- **Active.** You gain a pool of d4s equal to your level. You can spend 1 action to touch a creature (other than yourself) and restore [[Greater and Lesser Healing|Lesser Healing]] equal to the total rolled. You regain expended d4s on a [[Long Rest]].
### Mark of Endurance
- **Passive.** Choose a damage type besides physical. You can't gain [[Lingering Injury#Minor Injury Table|Minor Injuries]] from that damage type.
- **Active.** When you gain the [[01 Condition#Staggered|Staggered]] condition, you can last an additional round until you go [[01 Condition#Unconscious|Unconscious]].
### Mark of Exploration
 - **Passive.** You ignore [[Strained Movement]]. 
 - **Active.** Choose a creature you have seen within the last 24 hours. For the next hour, you gain [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]] on survival rolls to track the creature.
### Mark of Strength
- **Passive.** You gain a +2 bonus to Athletics checks and strength saving throws. 
- **Active.** You can choose to empower one of your weapon attacks. You must make this decision before making the attack. Increase the action cost of the attack by 1 and gain a +2 bonus to the attack roll. If the attack hits, forgo rolling the [[Weapon Dice]] of the attack and instead take the maximum value of each die as the damage. 

## 9th - Eyes of the Ascended 
You've arrived at the final transition of an inscribed orc, the Eyes of the Ascended. The most skilled inscribers in your tribe turn their craft to the most delicate task of all: tattooing the eyes themselves. Select the same Tribal Tattoo and Tribal Mark you chose at 1st and 5th level. Those features gain an additional effect determined by the combination of both. 

- **Bone + Mark of Faith:** You can now use your Mark of Faith active to deal [[Greater and Lesser Healing|Greater Healing]] to undead. 
- **Bone + Mark of Endurance:** You now also ignore [[Impeded Movement]]. Additionally while [[01 Condition#Staggered|Staggered]], you can choose to temporarily reduce your maximum HP by 10 to last an additional round while [[01 Condition#Staggered|Staggered]]. You cannot reduce your maximum HP below 1. You regain your lost maximum HP on a [[Long Rest]].
- **Bone + Mark of Exploration:** You can now use your Mark of Faith active to deal [[Greater and Lesser Healing|Greater Healing]] to undead. 
- **Bone + Mark of Strength:** Your resistance to necrotic damage is now equal to your level. Your empowered attacks from Mark of Strength also deals additional necrotic damage equal to your level.
- **Blessed + Mark of Faith:** Your resistance to radiant damage is now equal to your level. Your Mark of Faith healing pool increases to d6s instead of d4s.
- **Blessed + Mark of Endurance:** Choose an additional [[Lingering Injury#Minor Injury Table|Minor Injury]] besides physical for your Mark of Endurance. Your Blessed Tattoo ward now affects the next two attacks from the creature.
- **Blessed + Mark of Exploration:** You always know the direction of the nearest source of divine magic within 1 mile. Your tracking [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]] now applies to any creature you have encountered, not just within 24 hours.
- **Blessed + Mark of Strength:** Roll an additional damage die on rolls that deal radiant damage. Your empowered attacks from Mark of Strength also deals additional radiant damage equal to your level.
- **Glyph + Mark of Faith:** Your spellcasting focus tattoo now grants +2 to your spell save DC. The first time your Mark of Faith healing restores [[Hit Points]] to a target, they gain [[Temporary Hit Points]] equal to your level. They are then immune to this effect for 24 hours. 
- **Glyph + Mark of Endurance:** When you restore [[Hit Points]] to a creature with the [[01 Condition#Staggered|Staggered]] condition, you heal the maximum amount. 
- **Glyph + Mark of Exploration:** You can empower the spell granted by your Glyph by 1 level without spending the [[Mana]] cost once per long rest. You ignore [[Strained Movement]] and [[Impeded Movement]] while sustaining a spell.
- **Glyph + Mark of Strength:** Once per [[Long Rest]] when you cast a spell, you may follow it immediately with an empowered weapon attack as a free action.
