A corrupt race of orcs, long polluted by demon blood. Wrathful and tainted with an insatiable lust for blood.

## 1st - Corrupted Blood Lust
Whenever you are [[01 Condition#Bloodied (WIP)|Bloodied]], you gain a bonus to attack rolls and movement speed determined by your training.

- **Trained:** +1 to attack rolls and a +10 to movement speed.
- **Expert:** +2 to attack rolls and a +15 to movement speed. You also gain a +2 bonus to damage rolls. 
- **Master:** +3 to attack rolls and a +20 to movement speed. Increase the damage bonus to +3 and if you spend an action to [[Move]] during your turn, your weapon deals and [[Weapon Dice|Weapon Die]] of damage. 

## 5th - Bloodstained Channel
Your demonic lineage permeates through your magic or your attacks. When you gain this feat at 5th level, choose from **Fallen Infusion** or **Chaos Blood.**

- **Fallen Infusion.** You're spells are infused with extra occult energy. During your [[daily preparations]], choose up to 4 cantrips to become **infused.** If they deal damage, change their damage to bleed damage. They also deal persistent bleed damage on hit equal to your training. 
- **Chaos Blood.** You're weapons are infused with extra occult energy. During your [[daily preparations]], choose up to 4 weapons you have proficiency with to become **infused.** Regardless of the weapons damage type, it deals bleed damage and is considered a slashing weapon. They also deal persistent bleed damage on hit equal to your training. 

- **Trained:** 1d4 persistent bleed
- **Expert:** 1d6 persistent bleed
- **Master:** 1d8 persistent bleed

## 9th - Hell's Gate
You are tied to the infernal realms and can twist and manipulate the fabric between them. You can create rifts in-between this realm that you and your allies can traverse. You spend 2 actions to create a rift in an unoccupied space within 5 feet of you. You then create a second rift in an unoccupied space you can see within 60 feet. When you or any of your allies enters any of the rifts spaces, that character can teleport to any of the other rifts as a free action, as long as the destination zone is unoccupied. The rifts last for 1 minute.
