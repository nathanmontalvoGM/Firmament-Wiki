## Choosing your Ancestry 
Select an ancestry from list below.
- [[Dwarf]]
- [[Elf]]
- [[Gnome]]
- [[Goblin]]
- [[Human]]
- [[Lizardfolk]]
- [[Orc]]
Ancestries determine a number of your characters starting stats. 

- **[[Hit Points]].** Your ancestry contributes a set number of [[Hit Points]] on top of the [[Hit Points]] your class provides.
- **[[Movement Speed]].** Your starting movement speed is determined by your dexterity modifier and any bonuses or penalties your ancestry provides. 
- **Ability Score Increase.** Your ancestry contributes bonuses to your ability scores. 
- **Features.** Innate traits and abilities unique to your ancestry. 
- **Languages:** The languages your character speaks at character creation. Every character knows common but can learn an additional language for every bonus you have to intelligence, you learn an additional language.

Alternatively, there is a page on the wiki with a list of all the ancestries: [[Ancestries]].

## Choosing your Heritage
After selecting your ancestry, choose a heritage. Heritages represent a distinct bloodline or cultural background within your ancestry. Heritages allow for additional features at level 1 and further features at levels 5 and 9.

Each ancestry has its own list of available heritages. You select one heritage at character creation.
