At 1st level, when you create your character, you gain a background of your choice that represents your characters life before they go adventuring. Backgrounds in Firmament are built around modular components hat fit a unique path your character has.

Each background is comprised of 4 distinct background features. Your Origin, Upbringing, Occupation and Motivation define is how your character got to where they are now. Next to each feature will be a brief description of what that feature entails. These are merely suggestions s to what this feature broadly represents. Work with your GM if you have something outside the list of these suggestions. 

You are also encourages to think about aspects of your backstory outside of the ones listed here. What relationships did your character have (A mentor, siblings, an organization they were close with)? A defining event in their life like a loss of a loved one, exile or a grand achievement they made.

## Background Features
### Origin
Where you came from and what shaped your early life.
- **Urban.** Raised in a city or large settlement. You gain a permanent +1 bonus to rolls with Diplomacy and Deception.
- **Rural.** Raised in a small town, village, or farmstead. You gain a permanent +1 bonus to rolls with Survival and Animal Handling.
- **Wilderness.** Raised outside of civilization entirely. You gain a permanent +1 bonus to rolls with Survival and Nature.
- **Nomadic.** Raised traveling between places with no fixed home. You gain a +2 bonus to checks to travel in unfamiliar locations and to navigate. 
- **Underground.** Raised in tunnels, caves, or subterranean settlements. Your perception checks in darkness are not [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Hindered]].
- **Maritime.** Raised on or near the sea. You gain a +2 to Athletics checks to Swim. Additionally, you gain [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]] on d20 rolls to be [[01 Condition#Sickened|Sickened]] by rough seas. 
- **Magical.** Raised in another plane or around intense magic. Choose from Arcana or Occultism. You gain a permanent +1 bonus to rolls with that skill.

### Upbringing 
The social and economic circumstances of your early life.
- **Privileged.** Wealth, status, or noble birth. You gain a permanent +1 bonus to rolls with Diplomacy. You start with twice the normal amount of starting gold.
- **Common.** Ordinary working class background. You gain a +1 to any one skill of your choice reflecting your trade.
- **Impoverished.** Raised in poverty or on the margins of society. You gain a +1 to stealth checks. You can travel in slums twice as fast as others and can find shelter in them without rolling.
- **Institutional.** Raised by an organization (temple, military, guild, orphanage). You gain a +2 to checks related to your institution's area of expertise. You know the internal hierarchy and customs of similar organizations.
- **Isolated.** Raised apart from mainstream society. You are accustomed to loneliness and gain [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]] on saving throws for effects that would deal psychic damage. 
- **Criminal.** Raised within or adjacent to illegal activity. You gain +1 to sleight of hand checks. If the location is of a moderate size, you know of any criminal organizations or black market goods.

### Occupation
What you did before adventuring.
- **Unemployed.** 
- **Martial.** Soldier, guard, mercenary, gladiator. You gain +1 to Athletics checks and [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]] on checks to recall information about military organizations, tactics or formations
- **Scholarly.** sage, scribe, researcher, student. You gain [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]] on Recall Knowledge checks in one academic field of your choice. 
- **Crafts.** Blacksmith, carpenter, tailor, artisan. You gain a +2 to Crafting checks related to your chosen profession. You cannot critically fail on crafting checks.
- **Trade.** Merchant, sailor, courier, teamster. +2 to Diplomacy checks when bartering. You always know the approximate market value of common goods. 
- **Performance.** Singer, actor, acrobat, street performer. You gain a +1 to Performance checks. 
- **Service.** Servant, cook, healer, innkeeper. You gain a +1 to Insight checks. You are adept at reading what people want and can more easily tell when people want something.
- **Spiritual.** Acolyte, priest, cultist, monk. You gain a +1 to Religion checks. You gain [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]] on checks to recognize religious iconography, rituals, or customs
- **Criminal.** Thief, smuggler, con artist, assassin. You gain a +1 to stealth checks. You can more easily identify a buildings security weaknesses.
- **Labor.** Farmer, miner, lumberjack, fisherman. You gain a +2 to Athletics checks involving heavy lifting or sustained physical effort. You ignore the first level of [[01 Condition#Exhausted x|Exhaustion]] you get each day. 

### Motivation
What drives you to adventure.
- **Duty.** Obligation to a person, organization, or cause. Once per session when you act directly in service of your duty, you gain [[Favored]] on a d20 roll related to that service of duty.
- **Wealth.** The pursuit of coin and material comfort. Items your party discovers are 20% more.
- **Glory.** Fame, recognition, and renown. Once per session when you accomplish something notable in front of witnesses, you gain a +2 bonus to Charisma checks until the end of the [[Scene]].
- **Knowledge.** The pursuit of answers and understanding. Once per session when you encounter something you have never seen before, you may ask the GM one question about it without rolling.
- **Redemption.** Atoning for past failures or sins. Once per session when you act in a way that directly addresses your past failure, you gain [[Favored]] on a d20 roll related to that action.
- **Revenge.** Settling an old score. +1 to attack rolls and damage against the specific individual wronged you
- **Survival.** Staying alive is reason enough. Your character starts with 5 [[Temporary Hit Points]] every [[Long Rest]].
- **Belonging.** Searching for a place or people to call home. When you take a [[Long Rest]] with your friends and allies, you gain 1 [[Hero Points|Hero Point]].
- **Curiosity.** The world is interesting and you want to see more of it. Once per session when you investigate something unknown, you gain [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]] on the first Recall Knowledge or Perception check made to learn about it.
- **Faith.** You live in service to a deity or higher power. Once per session when you act in direct accordance with your deity's tenets, gain [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]] on one roll of your choice
- **Freedom.** Someone or something is holding you back. Once per session, when an effect attempts to restrain your physically or mentally, you gain [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]] on the roll.
