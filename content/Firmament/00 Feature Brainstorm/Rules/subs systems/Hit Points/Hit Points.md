[[Hit Points]] represent a character's life and blood, and whether they have taken any wounds. You’re starting [[Hit Points]] are determined by your Class, Ancestry and Constitution modifier. 

When you reach half your [[Hit Points]], you gain the [[01 Condition#Bloodied (WIP)|Bloodied]] condition.
When you drop to 0, you gain the [[01 Condition#Staggered|Staggered]] condition.
