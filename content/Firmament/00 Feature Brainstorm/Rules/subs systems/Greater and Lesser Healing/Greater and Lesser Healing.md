Healing both, magical and non magical, is separated into two categorizes: **Greater Healing** and **Lesser Healing.** When restoring [[Stamina]] and [[Hit Points]] with either, you split the healing between the two or pick one or the other. 

## Greater Healing
Can restore both [[Stamina]] and [[Hit Points]]. Major healing is also capable of restoring creatures at 0 [[Hit Points]].
## Lesser Healing
Only restores [[Stamina]] and [[Hit Points]] to creatures who don't have the [[01 Condition#Bloodied (WIP)|Bloodied]] condition. Lesser healing is incapable of restoring creatures at 0 [[Hit Points]].
