## Idea 1
just like in pathfinder, if you beat a DC by 10 you crit.
## Critical Damage
Unless other wise stated, critical hits on attack rolls and critical fails on saving throws always deal critical damage.

crit damage will be calculated differently however

you will take your regular damage for the attack and take its max. This means that all weapons should list their maximum damage beside them on the character sheet to make the math easier. You then roll your [[Weapon Dice]] again and add it to your maximum damage

## Saving Throws
Critically throwing on a saving throw you will take your regular damage for the attack and take its max.
