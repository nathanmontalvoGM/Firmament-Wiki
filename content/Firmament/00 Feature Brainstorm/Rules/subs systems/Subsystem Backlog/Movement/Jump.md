1 action
You take a short horizontal or vertical jump. Jumping a greater distance requires using the Athletics skill for High Jump or Long Jump

- **Horizontal Jump** up to 10 feet horizontally if your Speed is at least 15 feet, or up to 15 feet horizontally if your Speed is at least 30 feet. You land in the space where your Leap ends 
- **Vertical Jump** up to 3 feet vertically and 5 feet horizontally onto an elevated surface.
