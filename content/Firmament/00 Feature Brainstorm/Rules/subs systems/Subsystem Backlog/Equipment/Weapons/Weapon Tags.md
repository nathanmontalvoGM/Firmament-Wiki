| Trait             | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Agile**         | The multiple attack penalty you take with this weapon on the second attack is −4 instead of −5, and −8 instead of −10 on the third and subsequent attacks.                                                                                    |
| **Backstabber**   | When you attack a flat-footed creature, this weapon deals 1 additional precision damage.                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Backswing**     | You can use the momentum from a missed attack with this weapon to lead into your next attack. After missing with this weapon on your turn, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to your next attack with this weapon before the end of your turn. |
| **Concealable**   | This weapon is small and easy to hide. You gain [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)\|Favored]] on Stealth checks to conceal it.                                                                                                         |
| **Deadly**        | On a critical hit this weapon deals an additional [[Weapon Dice]] of damage.                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Disarm**        | This weapon is proficient at tripping opponents. You can use this weapon for Athletics checks to [[Disarm]] creatures. It gains a +2 bonus when used in this way.                                                                             |
| **Double Barrel** | This weapon has two barrels. Loading both barrels takes twice as long but you can fire both at once.                                                                                                                                          |
| **Fatal**         | The fatal die replaces the weapon's damage die on a critical hit, and you roll the fatal die twice.                                                                                                                                           |
| **Free Hand**     | This weapon is held in one hand but leaves your hand free.                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Finesse**       | You can use your Dexterity modifier instead of Strength on attack rolls with this weapon.                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Forceful**      | This weapon becomes more dangerous as you build momentum. The second time you attack with it on your turn, add a +2 bonus to damage. On the third and each subsequent attack add a +4  bonus.                                                 |
| **Grapple**       | You can use this weapon to Grapple with the Athletics skill.                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Hampering**     | This weapon impedes movement. Creatures hit by this weapon treat all terrain as [[Strained Movement]] until the end of their next turn.                                                                                                       |
| **Jousting**      | While mounted, if you moved at least 10 feet this turn before attacking, you deal an additional [[Weapon Dice]] of damage.                                                                                                                    |
| **Kickback**      | This weapon has a powerful kickback. You take a −2 circumstance penalty to attack rolls unless you have a Strength of 16 or higher.                                                                                                           |
| **Nonlethal**     | Attacks with this weapon are nonlethal and deal no damage to unconscious creatures.                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Parry**         | You can use this weapon to parry. While wielding it you can spend 1 action to gain a +1 circumstance bonus to AC until the start of your next turn.                                                                                           |
| **Propulsive**    | Add your Strength modifier (if positive) to damage rolls with this ranged weapon.                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Razing**        | This weapon is particularly effective against structures and objects. You deal an additional [[Weapon Dice]] of damage to structures and objects.                                                                                             |
| **Reach**         | This weapon has a range of 10 feet.                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Reload**        | The weapon must be reloaded between shots. The value indicates the number of Interact actions required.                                                                                                                                       |
| **Repeating**     | This weapon has a magazine and doesn't need to be reloaded until the magazine is empty.                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Shove**         | You can use this weapon to Shove with the Athletics skill.                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Sweep**         | This weapon makes wide sweeping attacks. On hit, deal damage to adjacent creatures equal to your strength bonus.                                                                                                                              |
| **Tethered**      | This weapon is attached to a rope or chain. After throwing it you can retrieve it as a free action.                                                                                                                                           |
| **Thrown**        | You can throw this weapon as a ranged attack.                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Trip**          | This weapon is proficient at tripping opponents. You can use this weapon for Athletics checks to [[Trip]] creatures. It gains a +2 bonus when used in this way.                                                                               |
| **Two-Hand**      | This weapon can be wielded with two hands. When wielded two handed the weapon uses the listed damage die instead of its normal die.                                                                                                           |
| **Undetectable**  | This weapon is magically hidden from detection.                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Versatile**     | This weapon can deal a different damage type by wielding it with 1 or 2 hands. The alternate damage type is listed in the trait.                                                                                                              |
| **Volley**        | This weapon is less effective at close range. Attacks against targets within the listed range are [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)\|Hindered]].                                                                                      |
