- **Tradition:** #DivineTradition 
- **Casting Time:** 1 minute
- **Range:** 60 feet
**Targets:** 1 creature
- **Components:** #Divination 
- **Casting Requirements:** #Verbal #Somatic #Material  
- **Duration:** 1 minute
**Stability:** #Unstable 

You attempt to magically discern the intentions of a creature within range. The target must make a wisdom saving throw vs your spell DC, the results of which are kept a secret. The potency of the spell is determined by the success of the target.

**Critical Success:** The target is aware you cast this spell on them and you get false information in return
**Success:** The spell has no effect and you get misleading information in return.
**Failure:** You can get a sense of the creatures motives and whether or not they are their own.
**Critical Failure:** You know the creatures true intentions and if those intentions are magically affected..
