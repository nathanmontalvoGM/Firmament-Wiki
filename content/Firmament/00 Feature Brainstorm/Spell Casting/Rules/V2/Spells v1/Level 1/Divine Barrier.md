- **Tradition:** #DivineTradition 
- **Casting Time:** 2 actions
- **Range:** touch
**Targets:** 1 creature
- **Components:** #Aegis 
- **Casting Requirements:** #Verbal #Somatic 
- **Duration:** 1 minute
**Stability:** #Stable

A divine shield is placed on the arms of your target. The target can use the [[Raise Shield]] action to instead raise this divine shield, gaining a +2 circumstance bonus to AC. It can [Shield Block](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=839) with its Raised arm as well; when it does, the target reduces the damage as if it had a shield with Hardness of 1d6. 

**At higher levels.** The hardness gains a +2 bonus to the damage blocked.
