- **Spell Level:** 2
- **Tradition:** #ArcaneTradition #PrimalTradition #OccultTradition #DivineTradition 
- **Casting Time:** 2 actions
- **Components:** #Fire, #Evocation
- **Casting Requirements:**  #Verbal #Somatic
- **Duration:** instantaneous
- 
You hurl a ray of flame. Make ranged spell attack against all targets within range. On hit, the target takes 3d6 fire damage and they take 1d4 persistent fire damage

**Critical Hit:** [[01 Critical Hits#Critical Damage|Critical Damage]] and the target takes 2d4 persistent fire damage.

**Empower:** the damage increases by 1d6 and the persistent damage increases by 1d4
