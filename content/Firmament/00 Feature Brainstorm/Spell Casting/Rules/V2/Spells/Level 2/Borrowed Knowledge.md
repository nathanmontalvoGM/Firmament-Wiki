- **Spell Level:** 2
- **Tradition:** #DivineTradition 
- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Components:** #Divination 
- **Casting Requirements:** #Somatic 
- **Duration:** 1 hour 

You draw on knowledge from spirits of the past. Choose one skill. For the spell's duration, you are [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]] on d20 rolls with that skill. The spell ends early if you cast it again.

**Empower:** choose an additional skill
