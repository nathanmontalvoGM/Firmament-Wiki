- **Spell Level:** 2
- **Tradition:** #ArcaneTradition #DivineTradition #PrimalTradition 
- **Casting Time:** 2 actions
- **Components:** #Fire #Evocation #Vitality
- **Casting Requirements:** #Verbal #Somatic #Material
- **Duration:** instantaneous 
You seal bleeding wounds with fire. Any target that has persistent bleed damage can choose to take 1 fire damage. If they do, they can repeat the save against the bleed damage with a DC of 10 instead of 15. 

**Empower:** Lower the new DC by 1 (minimum of 1) and they gain resistance 1 to bleed damage for 8 hours. Increase resistance by 1 for each empower.
