- **Spell Level:** Cantrip
- **Tradition:** #ArcaneTradition #OccultTradition 
- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Components:** #Divination 
- **Casting Requirements:** #Somatic 
- **Duration:** 1 Round
- 
You extend your hand and point a finger at a target in range. Your magic grants you a brief insight into the target’s defenses. Until the end of your next turn, you gain [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]] on your first attack roll against the target, provided that this spell hasn’t ended.

**Empower:** extend the duration by 1 round

**At Higher Levels:** This spell is automatically empowered by 1 level when you reach 5th level and once more at 9th level.
