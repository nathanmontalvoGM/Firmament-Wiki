- **Spell Level:** 1
- **Tradition:** #ArcaneTradition #PrimalTradition 
- **Casting Time:** 2 actions
- **Components:** #Electricity 
- **Casting Requirements:** #Somatic 
- **Duration:** Concentration, 1 Minute
- 
When you cast this spell, your fingers start crackling with energy. Make spell attack against all creatures within range. On a hit, the target(s) takes 1d12 lightning damage. On subsequent turns, you can spend 1 action to target one different creature within range and deal 1d12 damage to that creature. 

**Empower:** increase the lightning damage by 1d12.
