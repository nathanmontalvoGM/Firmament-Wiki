- **Spell Level:** 1
- **Tradition:** #ArcaneTradition #PrimalTradition 
- **Casting Time:** 2 actions
- **Components:** #Physical 
- **Casting Requirements:** 
- **Duration:** Instantaneous, varies
You conjure a slick coating of grease, choosing either an area or a single target. Regardless of which form you choose, if a creature affected by the grease takes fire damage while covered or standing in it, they take 1d4 persistent fire damage as the grease ignites.

- **Area.** All solid ground in the area is covered in grease. Each creature standing on the greasy surface must succeed on a Dexterity save or fall [[01 Condition#Prone|Prone]]. A creature moving onto the greasy surface must attempt the same saving throw. If a creature [[crawl]]s then they are unaffected.
- **Target.** One creature you target must make a Dexterity saving throw or become covered in grease. While afflicted in this way, creatures attempting to [[Grapple]] them are [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Hindered]]. If they critically fail a d20 roll using an item or weapon, they must make a DC 10 flat check or drop the item or weapon as it slips from their grasp. This includes spell casting focuses. 
**Empower:** Increase the persistent fire damage by 1d4. The Dexterity saving throw DC increases by 1.
