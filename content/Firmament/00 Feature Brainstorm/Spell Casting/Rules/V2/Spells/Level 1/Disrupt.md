You magically distract a number of creatures within range and turn its momentary uncertainty into encouragement for another creature. The target(s) must make a wisdom saving throw or have their next d20 roll be [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Hindered]]. You then choose another creature you can see (not including yourself). The chosen creatures next d20 roll within 1 minute is [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]]. A creature can be empowered by only one use of this spell at a time.

**Empower:** choose an additional creature to gain [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]].
