You touch a willing creature who isn't wearing armor, and a protective magical force surrounds it until the spell ends. The target's base AC becomes 14 + its Dexterity modifier. 

**Empower (2):** Increase the AC bonus by +1 and you gain a +1 bonus to all saving throws.
