- **Spell Level:** 1
- **Tradition:** #DivineTradition 
- **Casting Time:** 2 actions
- **Components:** #Radiant 
- **Casting Requirements:** #Verbal #Somatic #Material 
- **Duration:** 1 Round

A flash of light streaks toward a creature of your choice within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes 2d6 radiant damage, and the next attack roll made against this target before the end of your next turn has [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]]. 

**Empower:** the damage increases by 1d6
