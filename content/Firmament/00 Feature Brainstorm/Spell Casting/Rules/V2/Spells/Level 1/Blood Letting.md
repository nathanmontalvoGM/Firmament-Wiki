- **Spell Level:** 1
- **Tradition:** #OccultTradition 
- **Casting Time:** 2 actions
- **Components:** 
- **Casting Requirements:** #Verbal #Somatic #Material 
- **Duration:** #Instantaneous 
Tiny slug like creatures swarm over the targets body. The target must make a basic Constitution saving throw or take 2d4 bleed damage. 

**Failure.** They also take 1d6 persistent bleed damage. This damage is 2d6 if the creature has the [[01 Condition#Bloodied (WIP)|Bloodied]] condition. 
**Critical Failure** [[Critical Damage]] 

**Empower:** increase the damage by 1d4 and the persistent bleed by +2
