- **Spell Level:** 1
- **Tradition:** #ArcaneTradition #PrimalTradition #OccultTradition 
- **Casting Time:** 2 actions
- **Components:** #Physical 
- **Casting Requirements:** #Verbal #Somatic 
- **Duration:** Concentration, Varies
- 
Gravity intensifies in the area for a moment. Each target within range must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, they take 1d8 force damage and are knocked [[01 Condition#Prone|Prone]]. While the spell is sustained, the affected area becomes [[Strained Movement]] with a minimum 5-foot radius regardless of delivery. Deliveries that expand the spell's area, such as Aura or Cone, treat this 5-foot radius as their minimum size. Any object that isn't being worn or carried in the area requires a successful Strength check against your spell save DC to pick up or move

**Empower:** increase the force damage by 1d8 and minimum radius increases by 5 feet.
