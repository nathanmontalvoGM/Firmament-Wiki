- **Prerequisite:** [[Arcane Warrior]]
- **Level:** 6
- **Tag:** #Rune 
- **Type:** #RudimentaryFeat 

Your attacks exploit weakness in your opponents armor.

- **Expert:** On hit, the creature takes an extra 2d8 force damage. Until the start of your next turn, the creature is [[01 Condition#Off-Guard|Off-Guard]].
- **Master:** As expert, but the creature gains vulnerability to your next attack equal to your level to until the end of your next turn.
