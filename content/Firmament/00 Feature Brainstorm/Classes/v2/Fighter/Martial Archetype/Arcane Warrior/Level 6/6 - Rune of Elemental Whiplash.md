- **Prerequisite:** [[Arcane Warrior]]
- **Level:** 6
- **Tag:** #Rune 
- **Type:** #RudimentaryFeat 

Flavor Text.

- **Expert:** On hit, the creature takes an extra 2d8 elemental damage (choose acid, cold, fire, or electricity when you inscribe the rune). The next time the creature hits you before the start of your next turn, it takes 1d8 damage of the same type.

- **Master:** As Expert, but the reactive damage increases to 2d8.
