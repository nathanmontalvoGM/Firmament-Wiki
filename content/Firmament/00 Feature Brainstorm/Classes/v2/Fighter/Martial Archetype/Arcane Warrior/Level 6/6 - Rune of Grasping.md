- **Prerequisite:** [[Arcane Warrior]]
- **Level:** 6
- **Tag:** #Rune 
- **Type:** #RudimentaryFeat 

Flavor Text.

- **Expert:** On hit, the creature takes an extra 2d8 force damage. The creature must make a Constitution saving throw or be [[01 Condition#Slowed|Slowed]] until the start of your next turn.
- **Master:** As Expert, but on a failure the creature can only take 1 [[Move]] action until the start of your next turn.
