- **Prerequisite:** [[Arcane Warrior]]
- **Level:** 3
- **Tag:** #Rune 
- **Type:** #RudimentaryFeat 

Your attacks are coated with a magical noxious odor.

- **Trained:** On a hit the target takes an additional 1d8 poison damage and must succeed on a Constitution saving throw against your Class DC or become [[Sickened]].
- **Expert:** As Trained but the damage increases to 2d8.
- **Master:** As Expert. Additionally any creature other than you that starts its turn within 15 feet of the [[Sickened]] target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw against your Class DC or also become [[Sickened]].
