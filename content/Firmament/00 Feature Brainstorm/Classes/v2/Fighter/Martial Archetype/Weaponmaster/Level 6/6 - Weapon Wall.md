- **Prerequisite:** #Fighter [[Weaponmaster]]
- **Level:** 6
- **Tag:** #Aegis  #CombatExploit 
- **Type:** #RudimentaryFeat 

**Trigger:** You are targeted by a melee attack.
**Fundamental:** You interpose your Signature Weapon between yourself and the incoming strike. You gain a bonus to AC against the triggering attack equal to your training bonus. If the attack still hits, reduce the damage by an amount equal to one [[Weapon Dice]] of your Signature Weapon.

- **Trained:** +2 AC, reduce damage by 1 [[Weapon Dice]]
- **Expert:** +3 AC. Add your strength bonus to the damage reduced. If the attack misses as a result, you may immediately make one attack against the attacker as a reaction
- **Master:** +4 AC. If the attack misses as a result, your reaction attack gains [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]].
