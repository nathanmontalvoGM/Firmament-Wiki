- **Prerequisite:** #Fighter [[Weaponmaster]]
- **Level:** 3
- **Tag:** #Strike #CombatExploit 
- **Type:** #RudimentaryFeat 

**Trigger:** You make an attack with you're Signature Weapon

- **Trained:** The second attack gains a +2 to the attack rolls.
- **Expert:** The second attack gains a +3 to the attack rolls.
- **Master:** The second attack gains a +4 to the attack rolls. If the attack hits, it deals an additional [[Weapon Dice|Weapon Die]] of damage.
