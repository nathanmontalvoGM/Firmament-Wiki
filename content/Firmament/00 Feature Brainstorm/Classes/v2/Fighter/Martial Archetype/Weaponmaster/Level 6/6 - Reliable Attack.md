- **Prerequisite:** #Fighter [[Weaponmaster]]
- **Level:** 6
- **Tag:** #Strike #CombatExploit 
- **Type:** #RudimentaryFeat 

**Trigger:** You make an attack with a weapon you're Signature Weapon
**Fundamental:** If the attack misses, you can reroll the attack. You must take the new result. 

- **Trained:** as written
- **Expert:** As Trained. If the reroll hits, the attack deals an additional [[Weapon Dice]] of damage.
- **Master:** As Expert. If the reroll also misses, gain [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]] on your next attack.
