- **Prerequisite:** #Fighter  #Haste [[Weaponmaster]]
- **Level:** 9
- **Tag:** #Aegis  #CombatExploit 
- **Type:** #RudimentaryFeat 

**Trigger:** You move as a result of [[3 - Flashy Footwork]].
**Fundamental:** Your movement is an act of aggression. Enemies you move through or pass adjacent to during your free movement must make a Dexterity saving throw against your Class DC or suffer an effect based on your training. Additionally, if you end your free movement in a different space than you started, your next attack this turn ignores the multiple attack penalty.

- **Master:** On a failed save, the creature is [[01 Condition#Off-Guard|Off-Guard]] Until the start of your next turn , takes 2d6 of your [[Weapon Dice]] damage, and is pushed 5 feet in a direction of your choice.
