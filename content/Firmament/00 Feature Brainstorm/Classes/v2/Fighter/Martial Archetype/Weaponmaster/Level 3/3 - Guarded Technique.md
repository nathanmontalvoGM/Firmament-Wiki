- **Prerequisite:** #Fighter [[Weaponmaster]]
- **Level:** 3
- **Tag:** #Aegis #CombatExploit 
- **Type:** #RudimentaryFeat 

**Trigger:** You make an attack with a weapon you're Signature Weapon
**Fundamental:** After hitting with an attack, you gain a bonus to your AC determined by your training until the start of your next turn.

- **Trained:** +2 AC. 
- **Expert:**  +3 AC. You gain resistance 3 to the next weapon attack that would hit you.
- **Master:** +4 AC. You gain resistance 5 to the next weapon attack that would hit you. If the attacker is within range of your weapon, you can make an [[Opportunity Attack]] as a reaction.
