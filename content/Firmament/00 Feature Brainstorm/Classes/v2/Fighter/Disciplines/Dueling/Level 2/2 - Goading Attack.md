- **Prerequisite:** [[Dueling Discipline]]
- **Level:** 2
- **Tag:** #Strike #Maneuver 
- **Type:** #FlexibleFeat 
- **Trigger:** You are attacking your Duel Target.
- **Cost:** 1 action

**Fundamental:**
Make a melee attack. If the attack hits, the target must make a wisdom saving throw against your class DC. 

- **Trained:** On a failed save, the target cannot move more than half its movement speed away from you until the start of your next turn.
- **Expert:** As Trained. On a failed save the target also takes a −2 penalty to attack rolls against creatures other than you until the start of your next turn.
- **Master:** As Expert. On a failed save the target is also [[01 Condition#Off-Guard|Off-Guard]] against your attacks until the start of your next turn.
