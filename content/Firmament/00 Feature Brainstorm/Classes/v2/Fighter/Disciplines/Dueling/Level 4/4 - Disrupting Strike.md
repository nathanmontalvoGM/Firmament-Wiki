- **Prerequisite:** [[Dueling Discipline]]
- **Level:** 4
- **Tag:** #Strike #Maneuver 
- **Type:** #FlexibleFeat 
- **Trigger:** You are attacking your Duel Target.
- **Cost:** 1 Action

**Fundamental:**
Make a melee attack against your Duel Target. On a hit your strike disrupts the target's focus.

- **Trained:** The target must make a constitution saving throw. On a failure, the target's is [[01 Condition#Stunned|Stunned]].
- **Expert:** As Trained. The Constitution saving throw is [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Hindered]]
- **Master:** As Expert. The targets first d20 roll is [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Hindered]].
