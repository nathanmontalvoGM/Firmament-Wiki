- **Prerequisite:** [[Dueling Discipline]]
- **Level:** 6
- **Tag:** #Strike #Maneuver 
- **Type:** #FlexibleFeat 
- **Trigger:** Your Duel Target attacks a creature other than yourself.
- **Cost:** 1 Reaction

**Fundamental:**
You [[Move]] up to a set distance toward your Duel Target without provoking [[Opportunity Attack]]s. If you end this movement within melee range, you may make one melee attack against them.

- **Expert:** Move up to 15 feet.
- **Master:** Move up to your full movement speed.
