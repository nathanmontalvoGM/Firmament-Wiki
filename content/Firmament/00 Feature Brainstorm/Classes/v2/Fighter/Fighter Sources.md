## Arcane Warrior
### Level 2+
Brainstorm.
* Defense
* Utility
* Healing
* Damage
* Rune Alteration

#### Arcane Ward
Nearby allies are warded by attacks from this creature.
- **Trained:** One creature (including yourself) within 20 feet of the target creature gains a +2 to AC from that creatures next attack. 
- **Expert:** The range increases to 30 feet and the AC bonus increases to +3.
- **Master:** Any number of creatures within range can gain the benefits of Arcane Ward. The attack roll is also [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Hindered]]. 

#### Elemental Susceptibility
You create a vulnerability in your enemy.
- **Trained:** Choose a damage type (acid, cold, electricity, or fire). The target gains vulnerability 3 to that damage type for 1 minute. Applying this effect to a creature already affected by it does not stack. Instead, you may replace the existing damage type with a new one.
- **Expert:** Increase the damage vulnerability to 6. Additionally, you an choose to have the damage type of the rune changes to the damage type you chose. 
- **Master:** Increase the damage vulnerability to 10. Depending on the damage type chosen, the rune gains an additional effect. 
	- *Acid.* the target is [[01 Condition#Off-Guard|Off-Guard]] until the start of their next turn.
	- *Cold.* the target is [[01 Condition#Slowed|Slowed]] until the start of their next turn.
	- *Electricity.* the target gains [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Hindered]] on their next d20 roll. 
	- *Fire.* The target takes 1d8 persistent fire damage.

### Level 6+

#### Syphon Life

**Trigger:** you have the [[01 Condition#Bloodied (WIP)|Bloodied]] condition.
- **Expert:** If you deal damage with this rune, you regain 5 [[Temporary Hit Points]].
- **Master:** Increase the [[Temporary Hit Points]] gained to 8. While you have these [[Temporary Hit Points]], you gain a +2 bonus to AC. 

#### Swift Foot
- **Expert:** If you deal damage with this rune, you can [[Move]] up to half your movement speed.
- **Master:** This movement doesn't provoke reactions and you can [[Move]] up to your full movement speed.

#### Arcane Overload
- **Expert:** Once per turn when you deal damage with this rune, you may choose to overload it. The attack deals an additional 2d6 force damage but you take 1d6 force damage from the impact. This self damage can't be reduced in ignored in any way.
- **Master:** The damage increases to 3d6 and the self damage increased to 2d6. If the target of your rune gains the [[01 Condition#Bloodied (WIP)|Bloodied]] condition from taking this damage, you ignore the self damage. 

### Level 10+
#### Arcane Rend
- **Master.** When you deal damage with this rune, until the start of your next turn, all damage you deal ignores resistance and treats immunity as resistance equal to half the CR of the monster. Additionally, the next attack against the same target gains [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]]. 

#### Vital Surge
- **Master.** When you deal damage with this rune, you and all allies within 30 feet regain [[Hit Points]] equal to half the runes damage dealt as [[Greater and Lesser Healing|Greater Healing]].

#### Temporal Shift
- **Master.** When you deal damage with this rune, the target must make a Wisdom saving throw against your Class DC. On a failure, you can reposition them onto any stable surface you can see within 30 feet. The creature is then [[01 Condition#Dazzled|Dazzled]] until the end of their next turn. 
## Weapon Master
### Level 2+
#### Iron Discipline
Your training allows you to push beyond pain and exhaustion.

**Trigger:** While you are not [[01 Condition#Bloodied (WIP)|Bloodied]]:

**Trained**: Gain a +1 bonus to AC until the start of your next turn.
**Expert**: Gain a +1 bonus to AC and saving throws until the start of your next turn.
**Master**: Gain a +2 bonus to AC, saving throws, and attack rolls until the start of your next turn.

#### Hilt Strike

**Trigger:** You have not yet made an attack this turn.

- **Trained:** Your first attack this turn deals an additional 1d4 of the weapons damage.
- **Expert:** Increase to 2d4. The attack also ignores resistance to the weapon's damage type.
- **Master:** Increase to 3d4. 

#### Halt
**Trigger:** A creature moves into your signature weapons reach.

- **Trained:** The creature must make a Strength saving throw against your [[Class DC]] or be [[01 Condition#Slowed|Slowed]] until the end of their turn.
- **Expert:** As Trained. On a failed save the creature is also [[01 Condition#Off-Guard|Off-Guard]] until the start of their next turn.
- **Master:** As Expert. You may also move up to 10 feet as a free action.
