- **Prerequisite:** [[Barbarian]], [[Fury Instinct]]
- **Level:** 2
- **Tag:** #Strike #Rage 

**Trigger:** This attack is your first attack in combat

- **Target:** one creature 
- **Range:** melee range of your weapon
- **Action Cost:** 1
- **Duration:** #Instantaneous  

**Fundamental:**
You goad and provoke your opponent. Make a melee weapon attack. On hit, the target loses focus and anger clouds their judgment. You gain a bonus to attack rolls against this target until the end of your next turn determined by your training in this feat. 

- **Trained:** +2 bonus to attack rolls. The targets next attack is [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Hindered]].
- **Expert:**  +4 bonus to attack rolls. The target is [[01 Condition#Off-Guard|Off-Guard]] until the start of its next turn.
- **Master:** +6 bonus to attack rolls. The target must succeed on a wisdom [[saving throws]]. On a critical failure, their next attack must be against you.
