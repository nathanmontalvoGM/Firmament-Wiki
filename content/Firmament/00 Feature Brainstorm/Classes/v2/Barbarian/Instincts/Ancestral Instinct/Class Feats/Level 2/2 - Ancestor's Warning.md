- **Prerequisite:** [[Barbarian]], [[Ancestral Instinct]]
- **Level:** 2
- **Tag:** #Rage 
- **Type:** #RudimentaryFeat 

- **Target:** allies within 15 feet
- **Range:** 15 feet
- **Action Cost:** ---
- **Duration:** varies

**Fundamental:**
While your Ancestral Spirit is active, you and allies within 15 feet of it cannot be [[01 Condition#Off-Guard|Off-Guard]] against enemies they can see. 

- **Trained:** as fundamental
- **Expert:** increase the range to 20 feet
- **Master:** the range increases to 30 feet. Allies within range ignore [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Hindered]] on their first d20 roll.
