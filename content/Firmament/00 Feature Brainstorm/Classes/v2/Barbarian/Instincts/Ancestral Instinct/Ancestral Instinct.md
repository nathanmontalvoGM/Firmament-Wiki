## 1st Level - Ancestral Spirit (Instinct Ability)
At 1st level you can feel the presence of your ancestors. 
- **Target:** ---
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Action Cost:** 1 
- **Duration:** #Instantaneous  
- **Type:** #RudimentaryFeat 
While you are Raging you may summon one Ancestral Spirit as an action. The spirit is an incorporeal medium creature. It cannot be targeted by spells are interacted with/ This spirit  manifests in an unoccupied space within 30 feet of you and persists until you stop Raging or until it is dismissed as a free action. The spirit's power is determined by your current Temper.

**Ancestral Spirit**
The ancestral spirit can perform any of the following actions, moving is a free action once per turn. It can only perform one of these actions after you successfully hit with a melee attack.
* [[Move]] 20 feet.
* Deal 1d6 Force damage to a creature within 5 feet
* Grant an ally within 5 feet resistance 4 to physical damage.
* Restore 1d6 [[Greater and Lesser Healing|Lesser Healing]] to an ally within 5 feet.

For every 2 Temper you have, the Ancestral Spirit grows stronger
* Increase the movement speed by 5 feet
* The Force damage increases by 1d6
* The resistance increases by 2
* The [[Greater and Lesser Healing|Lesser Healing]] increases by 1d6

## 1st Level - Ancestral Wrath (Breaking Point Feature)
You lose control of your Ancestral Spirit. The spirit attacks the last creature it used an action on. That creature and all creatures within 10 feet of it must must make a basic strength saving throw or take 1d6 times your maximum temper force damage as the spirits body erupts violently back to the [[Firmament]]. Creatures who fail are also knocked [[01 Condition#Prone|Prone]] and are [[01 Condition#Frightened|Frightened]] for 1 round. In addition to being [[01 Condition#Off-Guard|Off-Guard]] and [[01 Condition#Weakened (Strength)|Weakened]], you also can't resummon your Ancestral Spirit for 1 round.  
## 3rd Level - Instinct Technique 
At 3rd level your ancestors presence bolsters others. 
- **Target:** all allies within range
- **Range:** 15 feet
- **Action Cost:** ---
- **Duration:** #Instantaneous  
- **Type:** #RudimentaryFeat 

Allies within range gains a bonus to their attack rolls determined by your training in this feat. 
- **Trained:** +2 
- **Expert:** +3
- **Master:** +4
## 7th Specialization Ability
Your ancestors have fully accepted you as their champion. You may now have two Ancestral Spirits active simultaneously while Raging. Each spirit can act independently after you successfully make an attack. 

## 9th Raging Resistance (Ancestral Ward)
Your ancestors shield you from harm. While Raging you gain resistance to force damage and psychic damage equal to your Barbarian level. Additionally once per round when an ally within 30 feet of your Ancestral Spirit would take damage, you may redirect up to half that damage to yourself as a free action
