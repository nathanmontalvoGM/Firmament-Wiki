- **Prerequisite:** [[Barbarian]], [[Ancestral Instinct]]
- **Level:** 4
- **Tag:** #Rage 
- **Type:** #RudimentaryFeat 

- **Target:** self
- **Range:** 
- **Action Cost:** 
- **Duration:** 

**Fundamental:**
During your [[daily preparations]], pick a skill you have training in. When you summon your Ancestral Spirit, the spirit can help you on the chosen skill. A number of times determined by your training, you can choose to gain [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]] on your next d20 roll with that skill.

- **Trained:** 3
- **Expert:** 4. You can pick 2 skills instead of 1.
- **Master:** 5. You gain 2 stacks of [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]] on the roll instead of 1.
