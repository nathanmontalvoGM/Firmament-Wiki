- **Prerequisite:** [[Barbarian]], 
- **Level:** 2
- **Tag:** #Aegis #Rage 
- **Type:** #FlexibleFeat 

- **Target:** An ally within 30 feet of your Ancestral Spirit takes damage.
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Action Cost:** 1 reaction
- **Duration:** #Instantaneous 

**Fundamental:** Your ancestors intercede to aid an ally. As a reaction reduce the damage the ally takes by your training. Your Temper also increases by 1.

- **Trained:** 1d6.
- **Expert:** 2d6.
- **Master:** 3d6.
