 - **Prerequisite:** [[Barbarian]], [[Ancestral Instinct]]
- **Level:** 4
- **Tag:** #Rage 
- **Type:** #RudimentaryFeat #Summon 

- **Target:** 
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Action Cost:** 1 action
- **Duration:** 

**Fundamental:**
When you use your Ancestral Spirit feature, you may choose to summon an Animal Spirit instead of a humanoid spirit. This replaces your Ancestral Spirit for that Rage. The Animal Spirit resembles a land creature of your choosing. It acts after you successfully hit with a melee attack and can perform one of its actions action per turn. Its movement is a free action once per turn. Choose one companion type when you summon the spirit. The spirit does not scale with Temper.

- **Trained:** Small animal. Move 20 feet. The spirit can perform one of the following actions:
	- **Maul:** Deal 1d8 physical damage to a creature within 5 feet.
	- **Intercept:** Reduce damage an ally within 5 feet takes by 1d6 + your Constitution modifier as a reaction.
	- **Brace:** Grant an ally within 5 feet a +2 bonus to AC until the start of your next turn.
- **Expert:** Medium animal. Move 25 feet. 
	- **Maul** knocks the target [[01 Condition#Prone|Prone]] on a failed Strength saving throw against your Class DC. The base damage increases by 1d8.
	- **Intercept** reduction increases to 2d6 + your Constitution modifier.
- **Master:** Large animal. Move 30 feet. All effects increase by 1 die.
	- **Maul:** The base damage increases by 1d8.
	- **Roar:** Once per Rage, all enemies within 10 feet must make a Wisdom saving throw against your Class DC or be [[01 Condition#Frightened|Frightened]]. They can repeat saving throw at the end of each of their turns.
	- **Intercept** reduction increases to 3d6 + your Constitution modifier.
	- **Brace:** Grant an ally within 10 feet a +3 bonus to AC until the start of your next turn.
