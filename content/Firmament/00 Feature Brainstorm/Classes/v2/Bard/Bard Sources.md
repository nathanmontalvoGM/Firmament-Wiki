## Level 2+ Sources
### Resonance
Once per scene, when you use this feat, choose a creature you can see, your performance lingers in their mind, inspiring others around them. The next time that creature uses the [[01 Condition#Inspired|Inspired]] condition, after its effects are resolved they gain an effect determined by your training in this feat

- **Trained:**  They can inspire another creature they can see as an action.
- **Expert:** As Trained. This source can be used twice per scene.
- **Master:** As Expert. The creature also regains the [[01 Condition#Inspired|Inspired]] condition after using it once. They then lose it as normal the second time it's used.

### Cadence
Once per scene, when you use this feat, choose a creature you can see, your rhythm and their actions synchronize in unison.
- **Trained:** If the target of this feat does a similar action to you after your turn (such as attacking with a rapier after you attack with your short sword, or using a skill you just used) the target gains a +2 bonus to that d20 roll. 
- **Expert:** As Trained but they are also [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]] on the roll. This source can be used twice per scene.
- **Master:** As Expert. If the target uses all three of their actions to perform the same actions you did, all d20 rolls on their subsequent turn are [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]]. 

### Harmony
Once per scene, when you use this feat, choose a creature you can seee, you inspire those nearest to them.
- **Trained:** If the target is within 15 feet of another [[Inspired]] creature, they gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls until the end of their next turn.
- **Expert:** The range increases to 30 feet and the bonus increases to +4. This source can be used twice per scene.
- **Master:** As Expert. Both the target and the nearby [[Inspired]] creature gain the bonus.
### Ballad
Once per scene, when you use this feat, choose a creature you can see, you weave a protective melody around them.

- **Trained:** The target gains [[Temporary Hit Points]]  equal to your Charisma modifier.
- **Expert:** As Trained but the [[Temporary Hit Points]] equal your twice your Charisma modifier + half your Bard level. This source can be used twice per scene.
- **Master:** As Expert. While the target has these [[Temporary Hit Points]] they gain a +4 bonus to attack rolls.

## Level 6+ Sources
### Refrain
Twice per scene, when you use this feat, choose a creature you can see, you repeat a motif that echoes a previous effect.

- **Expert:** You apply the effects of a different source that has been applied within the last hour as it it was applied to this feat. The target gains all its usual effects.
- **Master:** As Expert. The target can also use any 1 action feature as a free action.

### Dissonance
Twice per scene, when this source affects an enemy, your performance disrupts their focus.

- **Expert:** The target must make a Wisdom saving throw against your spell save DC or take a −2 penalty to all d20 rolls until the end of their next turn.
- **Master:** As Expert. On a failed save the penalty increases to −4 and the target cannot use reactions until the end of their next turn.

### Crescendo
Twice per scene, when you use this feat, choose a creature you can see, your performance builds to a powerful peak.

- **Expert:** The next Arcane Art check you make this turn automatically succeeds without rolling. 
- **Master:** As Expert but the roll is automatically a critical success instead of a regular success.

## Level 10
### Encore
Twice per scene, when this source affects a creature, the crowd demands more.
- **Master:** You may use your Performing Arts feature as a free action without expending one of its uses. The performance started this way lasts until the end of your next turn only.
