**Key Ability:** Intelligence 
- When you choose this class at 1st level, you gain an attribute boost to Intelligence.
## Maximum Health
**[[Hit Dice]]:** 1d6 per Wizard level
**[[Hit Points]]:** 6 + your Constitution modifier
You increase your maximum [[Hit Points]] by this number at 1st level and every level thereafter.
**[[Stamina]]:** 2 + your Dexterity modifier (Minimum of 1).
**[[Stamina|Recovery Dice]].** 1d2
You increase your [[Stamina]] by this number at 1st level and every level thereafter.

## Proficiencies
**Armor:** Trained in [[Unarmored Defense]]
**Weapons:** Trained in 3 [[simple weapons]] of your choice and [[unarmed attacks]]
**Tools:** None  
**Saving Throws:** Expert in Wisdom and Intelligence and trained in the other ones.
**Perception:** Trained in Perception
**Skills:** Trained in Arcana
Trained in a number of additional skills equal to 2 plus your Intelligence modifier
## Spell Shaper
At 2nd level, the delivery for all your spells have their mana cost reduced by 1 (minimum of 0).
- **Trained:** as written
- **Expert:** pick a delivery that costs 1 mana or more. Reduce its base cost to 0.
- **Master:** Cantrips are automatically empowered by 1 level higher. Pick a second delivery to have its base cost reduced to 0.
