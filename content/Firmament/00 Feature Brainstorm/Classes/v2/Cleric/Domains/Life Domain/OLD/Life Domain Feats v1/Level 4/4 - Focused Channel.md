- **Prerequisite:** [[Life Domain]] 
- **Level:** 4
- **Tag:** #Vitality #ChannelDivinity
- **Type:** #RudimentaryFeat

Your channel divinity now heals more effectively. You ignore the [[01 Condition#Bloodied (WIP)|Bloodied]] condition when a target uses Healers Mercy, allowing the target to be healed past half health. Your [[Hit Dice]] for this roll is determined by your training instead of being d8s. You also increase your maximum [[Hit Dice]] by your training. 

- **Trained:** d10s. You gain 1 additional maximum [[Hit Dice]].
- **Expert:** d12s.
- **Master:** You gain  an additional maximum [[Hit Dice]]
