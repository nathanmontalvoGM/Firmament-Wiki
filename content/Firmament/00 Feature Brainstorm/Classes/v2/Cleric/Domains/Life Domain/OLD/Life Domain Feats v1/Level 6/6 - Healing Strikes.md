- **Prerequisite:** [[Life Domain]], #Strike
- **Level:** 6
- **Tag:** #Strike #Vitality 
**Cost:** 2 Actions
- **Type:** #FlexibleFeat 

**Fundamental**
Make a melee attack with your favored weapon. On hit, roll a number of d8s determined by your training and heal that amount as [[Greater and Lesser Healing|Greater Healing]] to a creature within 30 feet. Once a creature can has received healing in this way, they are immune to the effect of Healing Strikes for 1 hour. 

- **Expert:** 2d8
- **Master:** 3d8. 
- **Legendary:** 4d8
