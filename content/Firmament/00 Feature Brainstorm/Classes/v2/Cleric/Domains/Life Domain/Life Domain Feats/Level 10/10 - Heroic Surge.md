- **Prerequisite:** [[Life Domain]], #Vitality2, #Strike1 
- **Level:** 10
- **Tag:** #Vitality 
- **Type:** #FlexibleFeat 


Your healing invigorates your allies. Once per scene, when you cast a spell that restores hit points to a single target and that target regains [[Hit Points]] as a result of that spell, you can choose to quicken that creature until the end of their next turn. While quickened in this way, the creature gains the following benefits:

 * +15 bonus to movement speed, 
 * all their attacks gain [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]] 
 * you add your spellcasting ability to their damage rolls.
