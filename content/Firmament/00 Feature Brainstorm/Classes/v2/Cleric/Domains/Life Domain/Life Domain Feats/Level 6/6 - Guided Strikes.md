- **Prerequisite:** [[Life Domain]], #Strike 
- **Level:** 6
- **Tag:** #Strike #Divination 
- **Type:** #FleibleFeat 

- **Target:** 1 creature you can see
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Action Cost:** 2
- **Duration:** 1 round

**Fundamental:**
Choose one ally within range. Until the start of your next turn the ally gains the following benefits determined by your training.

- **Trained:** The ally's attacks gain [[Favored]]. Their attacks increase their degree of success by one step on a 19 and 20.
- **Expert:** As Trained. The degree of success on 18,19 and 20. Additionally the ally may reroll one damage die per attack and must take the new result.
- **Master:** As Expert. The degree of success on 17, 18,19 and 20 You may now target two allies simultaneously with this feat. Additionally if either target critically hits while under this effect, they gain 10 [[Temporary Hit Points]].
