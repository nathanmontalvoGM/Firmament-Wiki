- **Prerequisite:** [[Life Domain]] 
- **Level:** 1
- **Tag:** #Strike 
- **Type:** #FlexibleFeat 

**Fundamental:**
Your deity’s grace doesn’t extend to your sworn enemies. When a levelled spell would restores [[Hit Points]], you can cause the spell to deal damage instead. Make a spell attack roll. The spell deals radiant **Strike Damage** when used in this way. 

**Inherited:** Sources applied to this feat apply to the spell its used with.

- **Trained:** You may convert the spell’s healing into radiant **Strike Damage** for one target even if the spell affects multiple. 
- **Expert:** If the spell would heal multiple targets, you may choose any number of them to take **Strike Damage** instead of healing.
- **Master:** When you use a spell this way, deal extra radiant damage equal to your spellcasting modifier to each affected target.
