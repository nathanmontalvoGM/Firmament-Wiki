- **Prerequisite:** [[Life Domain]], #Vitality3
- **Level:** 10
- **Tag:** #Vitality 
- **Type:** #FlexibleFeat 
- **Target:** 1 creature other than yourself
- **Range:** —
- **Action Cost:** —
- **Duration:** 1 minute


You lend your healing power to your allies. When you cast a spell that restores [[Hit Points]] to a single target other than yourself and that target regains [[Hit Points]] as a result of that spell, the target gains the following benefits:
* For the duration, they can cast [[00 Feature Brainstorm/Spell Casting/Rules/V2/Spells/Level 1/Heal|Heal]] as a 3rd level spell on a creature other then themself. They then lose the ability top cast this spell.
