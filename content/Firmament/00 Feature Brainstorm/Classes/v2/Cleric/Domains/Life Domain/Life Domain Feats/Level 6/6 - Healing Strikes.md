- **Prerequisite:** [[Life Domain]], #Strike
- **Level:** 6
- **Tag:** #Strike #Vitality
- **Type:** #FlexibleFeat 
- 
- **Target:** 1 creature you can see within range
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Action Cost:** 2 actions
- **Duration:** #Instantaneous 

**Fundamental**
Make a melee attack with your favored weapon. On hit, roll a number of **Vitality Dice** (d8s) determined by your training and heal that amount as [[Greater and Lesser Healing|Greater Healing]] to a creature within 30 feet. Once a creature has received healing in this way, they are immune to the effect of Healing Strikes for 1 hour. 

- **Expert:** 2d8
- **Master:** 3d8.
