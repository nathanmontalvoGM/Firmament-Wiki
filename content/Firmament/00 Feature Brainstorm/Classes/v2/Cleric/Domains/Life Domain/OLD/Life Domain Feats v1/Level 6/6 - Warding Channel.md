- **Prerequisite:** [[Life Domain]] #Aegis 
- **Level:** 6
- **Tag:** #Aegis 
- **Type:** #FlexibleFeat 

- **Target:** Allies affected by Channel Divinity
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Action Cost:** ---
- **Duration:** 1 round

**Fundamental:** Your Channel Divinity leaves a lingering divine ward on those it touches. When an ally benefits from your : Healer's Mercy, they gain benefits determined by your training in addition to the normal healing.

- **Trained:** The ally gains a +2 bonus to AC until the start of your next turn.
- **Expert:** As Trained. Additionally the ally gains resistance 3 to all damage until the start of your next turn.
- **Master:** As Expert. The resistance increases to 5. Additionally if the warded ally would be reduced to 0 Hit Points before the start of your next turn, the ward shatters to prevent it and they are instead reduced to 1 Hit Point and the ward ends. Once an ally gains this benefit, they can't gain the benefit again for 1 hour.
