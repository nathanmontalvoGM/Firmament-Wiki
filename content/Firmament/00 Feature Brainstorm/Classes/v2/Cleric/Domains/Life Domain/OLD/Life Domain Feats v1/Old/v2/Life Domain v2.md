## Doctrine: Weave Essence
At 2nd level, your doctrine allows you to manipulate the healing energy around you. 

Whenever you restore hit points to a creature or a creature within 15 feet of you takes damage, you gain 1 Essence (to a maximum equal to your spellcasting ability).

Essence can be spent to fuel and enhance your abilities or others.

## Blessed Healer
Beginning at 4th level, the healing spells you cast on others heal you as well. When you cast a spell of 1st level or higher that restores hit points to a creature other than you, you regain hit points equal to 2 + your cleric level.

## Strengthened Essence Emanation
At 6th level, the range of your Weave Essence Doctrine feature increases to 30 feet. In addition, your maximum essence increases by 2.

## Empowered Essence
At 9th level, you draw more life essence from the magic around you. You now gain 2 essence instead of 1 whenever you restore hit points to a creature or a creature with takes damage within range of Weave Essence.

redirection of healing power into into new ways
#### What unique aspect does this subclass emphasize?
**Utility:** Healing and providing buffs to your healing and additional uses for them.  
**Damage:** Becomes a weaker paladin with the ability to smite with less damage and enable others to deal more damage. 
**Self Sustain:** Become a pseudo tank for your team, not by damage reduction or increased AC but by raw healing
