- **Prerequisite:** [[Life Domain]]
- **Level:** 4
- **Tag:** #Strike 
- **Type:** #RudimentaryFeat '

- **Target:** 1 creature you can see 
- **Range:** self
- **Action Cost:** 2
- **Duration:** 1 minute

**Fundamental:**
You gain [[Temporary Hit Points]] determined by your training. For the duration, you gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls with your Favored Weapon and any enemy that ends its turn within 10 feet of you, takes damage determined by your training. You are then immune to the effects of Aspect of Wrath for 1 hour. 

- **Trained:** You gain 10 [[Temporary Hit Points]] and enemies take 5 Radiant **Strike Damage**. 
- **Expert:** The [[Temporary Hit Points]] increases to 15 and the **Strike Damage** increases to 8.
- **Master:** The [[Temporary Hit Points]] increases to 20 and the **Strike Damage** increases to 10.
