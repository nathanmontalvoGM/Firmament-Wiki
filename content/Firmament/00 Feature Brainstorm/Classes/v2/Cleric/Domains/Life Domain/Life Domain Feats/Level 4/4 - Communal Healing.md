- **Prerequisite:** [[Life Domain]] 
- **Level:** 4
- **Tag:** #Vitality 

- **Type:** #RudimentaryFeat 

Flavor Text. When you cast a healing spell that targets one creature, you can heal another creature within 30 feet of the original target for an additional amount healing determined by your training. 

- **Trained:** You heal 1 **Vitality Die** (d8) as [[Greater and Lesser Healing|Greater Healing]]
- **Expert:** The healing increases to 2d8 and the range increases to 60 feet.
- **Master:** You can heal an additional creature.
