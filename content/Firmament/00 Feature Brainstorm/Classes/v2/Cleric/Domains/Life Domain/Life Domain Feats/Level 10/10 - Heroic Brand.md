- **Prerequisite:** Life Domain, #Strike3 
- **Level:** 10
- **Tag:** #Strike #Vitality 
- **Type:** #FlexibleFeat
- **Target:** 1 creature other than yourself
- **Range:** —
- **Action Cost:** —
- **Duration:** Until the end of the target's next turn

**Fundamental:** Divine flavor. Once per scene, when you cast a spell that restores [[Hit Points]] to a single target other than yourself and that target regains [[Hit Points]] as a result, they gain the following benefits.

- Vitality Dice
- Their attacks deal radiant **Strike Damage**. This damage ignores resistances and immunities to radiant damage. 
- Their attacks deal an additional [[Weapon Dice|Weapon Die]] of damage.
- Critical hits [[01 Condition#Blinded|Blind]] the enemy until the end of their next turn.
