- **Prerequisite:** [[Life Domain]] 
- **Level:** 1
- **Tag:** #Vitality
- **Type:** #FlexibleFeat 

**Fundamental:** Whenever you restore [[Hit Points]] with a #Vitality spell, the healing is modified by your training in this feat.

**Inherited:** Sources applied to this feat apply to the spell its used with.

- **Trained:** When rolling **Vitality Dice**, treat any die rolls of 3 or lower as a 4 instead.
- **Expert:** You add 1 **Vitality Die** to the roll. Increase all **Vitality Dice** rolled by 1 step.
- **Master:** You add 2 **Vitality Die** to the roll instead of 1. Once per scene, you can remove a condition afflicting the creature.
