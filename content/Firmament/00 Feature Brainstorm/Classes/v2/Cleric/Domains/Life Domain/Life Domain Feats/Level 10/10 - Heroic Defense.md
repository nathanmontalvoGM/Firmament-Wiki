- **Prerequisite:** Life Domain, #Vitality2, #Aegis1
- **Level:** 10
- **Tag:** #Vitality #Aegis
- **Type:** #FlexibleFeat
- **Target:** 1 creature other than yourself
- **Range:** —
- **Action Cost:** —
- **Duration:** Until the end of the target's next turn

**Fundamental:** Your healing provides temporary divine protection to those you restore. Once per scene, when you cast a spell that restores [[Hit Points]] to a single target other than yourself and that target regains [[Hit Points]] as a result, they gain a +5 **Aegis Bonus** to AC and all saving throws until the end of their next turn.

**Inherited:** Sources applied to this feat apply to the spell it is used with.
