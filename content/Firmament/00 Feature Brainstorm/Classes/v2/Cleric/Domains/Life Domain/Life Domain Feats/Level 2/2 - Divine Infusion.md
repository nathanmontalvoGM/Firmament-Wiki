- **Prerequisite:** [[Life Domain]] 
- **Level:** 2
- **Tag:** #Vitality #Strike 
- **Type:** #FlexibleFeat 

- **Target:** Creature you resorted hit points to

**Fundamental:**
Once per turn, when you restores 5 [[Hit Points]] or more to a creature, that creatures next attack deals extra damage determined by your training.

- **Trained:** 1d6 radiant **Strike Damage**
- **Expert:** 2d6 radiant **Strike Damage**
- **Master:** 3d6 radiant **Strike Damage**. The attack is [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]].
