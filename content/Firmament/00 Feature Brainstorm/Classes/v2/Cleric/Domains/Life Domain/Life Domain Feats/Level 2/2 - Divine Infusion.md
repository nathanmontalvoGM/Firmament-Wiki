- **Prerequisite:** [[Life Domain]] 
- **Level:** 2
- **Tag:** #Vitality #Strike 
- **Type:** #FlexibleFeat 

- **Target:** Creature you resorted hit points to

**Fundamental:**
When you restores 5 [[Hit Points]] or more to a creature, that creatures next attack deals extra damage determined by your training.

**Inherited:** Sources and Behaviors applied to this feat apply to the spell its used with.

- **Trained:** 1d8 radiant damage
- **Expert:** 2d8 radiant damage
- **Master:** 3d8 radiant damage. The attack is [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]].
