- **Prerequisite:** [[Cleric]], [[Martyr Domain]]
- **Level:** 4
- **Tag:** #Vitality #Strike 
- **Type:** #RudimentaryFeat 

- **Target:** 1 creature
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Action Cost:** ---
- **Duration:** #Instantaneous 
- **Trigger:** You are targeted by an effect that restores [[Greater and Lesser Healing|Greater Healing]].

**Fundamental:** Instead of regaining Hit Points from the triggering effect you absorb the healing and redirect it outward. The triggering effect rolls two additional dies of healing and the total is stored as a healing pool. This pool expires at the end of your next turn if unused. You may draw from this pool in one of two ways:

- **Draw for Healing:** Add the stored pool to your next #Vitality spell as [[Greater and Lesser Healing|Greater Healing]].
- **Draw for Damage:** Add the stored pool to your next spell as radiant damage. You take half that damage yourself. This damage cannot be reduced in ay way.

- **Trained:** as written
- **Expert:** As Trained. The pool now expires at the end of your second turn instead of your next turn. Additionally you may split the pool between both uses, spending part as healing and part as damage in separate actions before it expires.
- **Master:** As Expert. The regardless of the die value of the healing, it is rolled as a d12. Additionally the self damage from Draw for Damage cannot exceed 15 damage.
