- **Prerequisite:** [[Martyr Domain]]

- **Target:** self
- **Range:** touch
- **Action Cost:** ---
- **Duration:** #Instantaneous 

Your self-inflicted suffering fuels holy wrath. 
**Fundamental:**
Once per turn when you spend [[Hit Points]] using Blood Offering or take damage from your own spells or effects, your next melee attack gains additional effects determined by your training. 

- **Trained:** The target takes persistent bleed damage equal to the [[Hit Points]] spent (to a maximum of 5).
- **Expert:** Increase the maximum persistent bleed damage to 10. The target is crippled by the attack. They must make constitution save against your spell save DC or be [[01 Condition#Slowed|Slowed]] for 1 minute. They can repeat this save at the end of each of their turns. 
- **Master:** Increase the maximum persistent bleed damage to 15. While bleeding in this way, the target is [[01 Condition#Off-Guard|Off-Guard]].
