- **Prerequisite:** [[Martyr Domain]], #Strike2 
- **Level:** 10
- **Tag:** #Strike 
- **Type:** #FlexibleFeat 

- **Target:** 1 creature within reach of your melee weapon
- **Range:** melee
- **Action Cost:** 2 actions
- **Duration:** #Instantaneous 

**Fundamental:** Your wounds manifest on your enemy. Make a melee weapon attack. On a hit your injuries physically appear on the target. For every condition, Minor and Severe injury you are currently suffering, the target takes additional damage and gains a detrimental effect determined by your training.

- **Master:** For each condition or injury you are suffering the target takes an additional 2d6 radiant damage. Additionally the target gains a condition afflicting you. You choose the condition. Regardless of the conditions duration, it lasts until the end of the targets next turn.
