- **Prerequisite:** [[Martyr Domain]]. #Vitality 
- **Level:** 6
- **Tag:** #Strike 
- **Type:** #FlexibleFeat 

- **Target:** 1 creature within range
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Action Cost:** 2 actions
- **Duration:** 1 minute

**Fundamental:** You sear a divine brand into your enemy, marking them for punishment. The brand deals ongoing damage determined by your training. 

- **Expert:** The target takes 2d6 radiant damage immediately and takes 1d6 persistent radiant damage for the duration. While they have persistent damage, you gain weakness 3 to their attacks. While you are [[Bloodied]], this ongoing damage increases to 2d6.
- **Master:** The initial damage increases to 3d6. The persistent radiant damage increases to 2d6 normally and 3d6 while you are [[Bloodied]] and the weakness increases to 5. Additionally while branded, the target cannot benefit from damage resistance.
