- **Prerequisite:** [[Cleric]], [[Martyr Domain]], #Aegis1 
- **Level:** 6
- **Tag:** #Aegis 
- **Type:** #FlexibleFeat 

- **Target:** —-
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Action Cost:** 1
- **Duration:** 1 minute
You raise your shield and it erupts in blinding divine light, searing those who dare approach.
**Fundamental:**

You conjure a massive blazing divine shield that hovers within 60 feet of you. The shield is 5 feet wide and 10 feet tall. Once per turn as an action, you may move the shield up to 30 feet.

Allies that are behind the shield and within 5 feet of it gain [[Cover|three-quarters cover]]. Enemies within 10 feet of the shield are [[01 Condition#Dazzled|Dazzled]] by its blinding light and must make a basic Constitution saving throw against your spell save DC or take radiant damage equal to your training. Creatures may use the [[Avert Gaze]] action to avoid the [[01 Condition#Dazzled|Dazzled]] effect.

- **Expert:** 2d8 radiant damage
- **Master:** 3d8 radiant damage, and the shield counts as [[Cover|Total Cover]] instead of [[Cover|three-quarters cover]]
