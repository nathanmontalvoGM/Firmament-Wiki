- **Prerequisite:** [[Martyr Domain]], #Strike1 
- **Level:** 8
- **Tag:** #Strike 
- **Type:** #RudimentaryFeat 
- 
- **Target:** All allies within range
- **Range:** 15 foot aura centered on you
- **Action Cost:** 2 actions
- **Duration:** varies

**Fundamental:** You project an aura of divine conviction that sharpens your allies' strikes. While this aura is active all allies within 15 feet gain [[Favored]] on their first attack roll each turn. You may dismiss this aura as a free action.

- **Expert:** As Fundamental. Additionally when an ally within the aura scores a [[Critical Hits|Critical Hit]] on their first attack roll, you may choose as a free action to take vulnerability up to 15 to the next attack that hits you. If you do the ally that scored the critical hit deals bonus damage equal to twice the vulnerability value on that attack.
- **Master:** The aura range increases to 30 feet. Allies gain two stacks of [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]] instead of one.
