- **Prerequisite:** [[Martyr Domain]], #Vitality1 
- **Level:** 8
- **Tag:** #Vitality 
- **Type:** #RudimentaryFeat 

- **Target:** All allies within range
- **Range:** 15 foot aura centered on you
- **Action Cost:** 2 actions
- **Duration:** varies

**Fundamental:** Your blood heals those close to you. 2 actions. At the start of each of your turns allies within 15 feet regain Hit Points and you gain weakness to all damage determined by your training. The healing increases if your are [[01 Condition#Bloodied (WIP)|Bloodied]]. You can dismiss this aura as a free action.
- **Expert:** Allies regain 1d6 Hit Points as [[Lesser Healing]]. While you are [[Bloodied]] this increases to 1d8 [[Greater Healing]]. You gain weakness 4 to all damage.
- **Master:** The aura range increases to 30 feet. Allies regain 2d8 [[Greater and Lesser Healing|Lesser Healing]] normally and 2d10 [[Greater and Lesser Healing|Greater Healing]] while [[01 Condition#Bloodied (WIP)|Bloodied]]. Additionally once per [[Long Rest]] when an ally within the aura would be reduced to 0 Hit Points they instead drop to 1 Hit Point.
