- **Prerequisite:** [[Cleric]], [[Martyr Domain]]
- **Level:** 2
- **Tag:** #Vitality 
- **Type:** #FlexibleFeat 

- **Target:** target(s) of the spell
- **Range:** ---
- **Action Cost:** ---
- **Duration:** ---
- **Requirement:** you are healing a creature with the [[01 Condition#Bloodied (WIP)|Bloodied]] condition.

**Fundamental:** When you cast a spell with the #Vitality component, and you are healing a creature with the [[01 Condition#Bloodied (WIP)|Bloodied]] condition, you heal them an additional amount determined by your training. The healing still applies even if it would remove the [[01 Condition#Bloodied (WIP)|Bloodied]] condition.

**Inherited:** Sources applied to this feat apply to the spell its used with.

- **Trained:** The target regains additional [[Hit Points]] equal to your Wisdom modifier. 
- **Expert:** Roll additional die of healing for the spell. 
- **Master:** You reroll 1s and 2s for all healing dice rolled.
