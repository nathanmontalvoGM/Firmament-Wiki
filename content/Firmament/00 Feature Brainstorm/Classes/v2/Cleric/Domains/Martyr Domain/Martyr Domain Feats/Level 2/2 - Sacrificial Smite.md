- **Prerequisite:** [[Cleric]], [[Martyr Domain]]
- **Level:** 2
- **Tag:** #Strike 
- **Type:** #FlexibleFeat 

- **Target:** 1 creature hit with a melee attack
- **Range:** melee
- **Action Cost:** 1 action
- **Duration:** #Instantaneous 

**Fundamental:**
Make a melee weapon attack. On a hit, you may spend up to 10 [[Hit Points]] to deal additional radiant damage equal to the [[Hit Points]] spent plus an amount determined by your training. 

- **Trained:** You deal an additional [[Weapon Dice]] of radiant damage for every 5 [[Hit Points]] spent. 
- **Expert:** Increase the maximum [[Hit Points]] you can spend to 20. If you spend 15 [[Hit Points]], increase all [[Weapon Dice]] by 2 steps 
- **Master:**  Increase the maximum [[Hit Points]] you can spend to 30. If this attack would inflict the [[01 Condition#Bloodied (WIP)|Bloodied]] condition, the target gains weakness to radiant damage equal to half the damage dealt.
