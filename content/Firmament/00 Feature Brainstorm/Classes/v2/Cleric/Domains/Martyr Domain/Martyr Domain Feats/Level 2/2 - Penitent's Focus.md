- **Prerequisite:** [[Cleric]], [[Martyr Domain]]
- **Level:** 2
- **Tag:** #Vitality 
- **Type:** #RudimentaryFeat 

- **Target:** self
- **Range:** touch
- **Action Cost:** ---
- **Duration:** 1 minute.

**Fundamental:**
When you spend [[Hit Points]] using Blood Offering, you gain the following benefits determined by your training.

- **Trained:** You and one ally within 30 feet gain a bonus to the next saving throw they make equal to your wisdom modifier.
- **Expert:** You and up to two allies within 30 feet gain the bonus. If an ally succeeds on a saving throw while benefiting from this effect, they regain [[Hit Points]] equal to twice your Wisdom modifier.
- **Master:** All allies within 60 feet gain the bonus.
