- **Prerequisite:** [[Martyr Domain]]
- **Level:** 4
- **Tag:** #Vitality 
- **Type:** #RudimentaryFeat 

- **Target:** 1 creature within range that you can see.
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Action Cost:** 1 reaction
- **Duration:** #Instantaneous 

**Fundamental:** Each wound you receive becomes a conduit for divine healing. When you take damage, you may immediately channel the damage as healing for your allies.

- **Trained:** One ally within 30 feet regains [[Hit Points]] equal to half the damage you took as [[Greater and Lesser Healing|Greater Healing]].
- **Expert:** You may now target up to two allies simultaneously splitting the healing between them as you choose.
- **Master:** The healing equals the full damage taken rather than half. Additionally if the damage reduces you to the [[Bloodied]] condition, you regain a spell slot.
