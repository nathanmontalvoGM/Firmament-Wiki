- **Prerequisite:** Cleric, Martyr Domain, #Aegis1
- **Level:** 8
- **Tag:** #Aegis
- **Type:** #RudimentaryFeat
- 
- **Target:** All allies within range
- **Range:** 15 foot aura centered on you
- **Action Cost:** 1 action to activate
- **Duration:** 1 minute

**Fundamental:** You wrap your allies in a shroud of divine protection. Allies within 15 feet gain damage resistance determined by your training. The resistance increases when you are [[01 Condition#Bloodied (WIP)|Bloodied]].
- **Expert:** Allies gain resistance 3 to all damage. While you are [[01 Condition#Bloodied (WIP)|Bloodied]] the resistance increases to 6.
- **Master:** The aura range increases to 30 feet Allies gain resistance 6 to all damage. While you are [[01 Condition#Bloodied (WIP)|Bloodied]] the resistance increases to 10. Additionally allies within the aura cannot be [[01 Condition#Stunned|Stunned]] or put [[01 Condition#Off-Guard|Off-Guard]].
