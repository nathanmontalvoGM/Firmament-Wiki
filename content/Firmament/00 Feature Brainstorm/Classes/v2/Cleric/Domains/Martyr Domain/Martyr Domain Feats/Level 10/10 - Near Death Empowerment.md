- **Prerequisite:** [[Cleric]], [[Martyr Domain]], #Vitality2 
- **Level:** 10
- **Tag:** #Vitality 
- **Type:** #RudimentaryFeat 

- **Target:** self
- **Range:** touch
- **Action Cost:** ---
- **Duration:** varies

**Fundamental:**
While you are at or below one quarter of your maximum Hit Points (rounded down), all #Vitality spells you cast roll an additional healing die and are automatically empowered by one level with no [[Mana]] cost. 

- **Master:** You may reroll any healing die with a result 1 or 2 and must use the new result.
