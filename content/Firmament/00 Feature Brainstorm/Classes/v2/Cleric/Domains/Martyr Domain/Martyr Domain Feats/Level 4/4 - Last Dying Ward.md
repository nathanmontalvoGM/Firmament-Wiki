- **Prerequisite:** [[Cleric]]. [[Martyr Domain]]
- **Level:** 4
- **Tag:** #Aegis 
- **Type:** #RudimentaryFeat 

- **Target:** self
- **Range:** ---
- **Action Cost:** ---
- **Duration:** varies
- 
- **Trigger:** You have 20 [[Hit Points]] or less.
- The closer you are to the death, the stronger your devotion becomes, repelling blade and spell.
**Fundamental:**
You gain bonuses determined by your training. Theses bonuses go away if you are healed above 20 [[Hit Points]], become [[01 Condition#Staggered|Staggered]] or go [[01 Condition#Unconscious|Unconscious]]. 

- **Trained:** You gain a +3 bonus to AC. For every missed attack against you receive while you have this bonus, you can give an ally you can see within 30 feet resistance 5 to the next source of damage they take. 
- **Expert:** You gain these benefits while under 30 [[Hit Points]]. The resistance increases to 8.
- **Master:** The AC bonus increases to +4 and the resistance increases to 10.
