## Cleric
### Life Domain
#### Level 2+

##### Life

Once per scene, when this feat affects a creature, you may restore a number of their [[Hit Dice]] determined by your training in this feat.

- **Trained:** Restore 2 [[Hit Dice]] + your Cleric level.
- **Expert:** Restore 3 [[Hit Dice]] + your Cleric level. This source can be used twice per scene instead of once.
- **Master:** Restore 4 [[Hit Dice]] + your Cleric level. You may also remove any one condition afflicting the creature.

##### Mercy

Once per scene, when this feat affects a creature, you or the creature can attempt to remove a negative condition afflicting them.

- **Trained:** The target may immediately attempt a saving throw against the afflicting condition as a free action.
- **Expert:** The target gains [[Favored]] on this saving throw. This source can be used twice per scene instead of once.
- **Master:** The condition is removed automatically without requiring a saving throw.

##### Sanctuary

Once per scene, when this feat affects a creature, you shroud them in divine protection, granting a bonus to AC. This effect ends when the target is hit or missed by an attack.

- **Trained:** The target gains +3 AC.
- **Expert:** The target gains +4 AC. This source can be used twice per scene instead of once.
- **Master:** The target gains +5 AC. The effect now persists for 2 attacks before ending.

#### Level 6+
##### Persistence

Twice per scene, when this feat affects a creature, you may extend the duration of the triggering feat. This source has no effect on feats with no listed duration.

- **Expert:** Extend the duration by 2 rounds.
- **Master:** Extend the duration by 2 rounds. Once per scene you may also ignore the first attempt to end the effect early.

##### Providence

Once per scene, when this feat affects a creature, divine providence watches over them.

- **Expert:** Until the end of your or the target's next turn, if they fail or critically fail a d20 roll they may reroll it and must take the new result.
- **Master:** The target gains [[Favored]] on all saving throws until the end of your or the target's next turn. This source can be used twice per scene instead of once.

#### Level 10+

##### Judgement

Twice per scene, when this feat affects a creature, mark one enemy you can see with a Mark of Judgement. The next ally to deal damage to that enemy gains an additional effect determined by your training.

- **Master:** The ally deals an additional 10 radiant damage. The die step of all damage dice rolled against the marked enemy increases by one step, to a maximum of a d12.

##### Zeal

Twice per scene, when this feat affects a creature, the next time that creature deals damage before the end of its next turn, it or one ally within 15 feet regains [[Hit Points]]

- **Master:** The chosen creature regains 6d8 [[Hit Points]]
##### Consecration

Twice per scene, when this feat affects a creature, you sanctify the ground around them.

- **Master:** Create a 15-foot consecrated area centered on the target until the end of its next turn. Allies within the area gain a +4 bonus to AC and resistance 7 to all damage types.
