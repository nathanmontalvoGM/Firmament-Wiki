## Cleric
* When making a feat about strike damage, the feat should account for die rolls as well as flat bonus to damage.
* sources can only be used once per turn
### Life Domain
#### Level 2+

##### Life
**Tag:** #Vitality 
When this feat affects a creature, add **Vitality Dice** to the feat's healing roll.

- **Trained:** Add 1 Vitality Die.
- **Expert:** The target regains 1 additional [[Hit Points|Hit Point]] for every 5 [[Hit Points]] restored.
- **Master:** Add 2 Vitality Dice instead of 1. The target also gains [[Temporary Hit Points]] equal to twice your Wisdom modifier that last until the start of your next turn.
##### Bolster
**Tag:** #Vitality 
When this feat affects a creature, you provide bonuses for 1 round.
- **Trained:** The targets movement speed increases by 10 feet.
- **Expert:** The target gains a +3 to damage rolls
- **Master:** The target gains a +3 to attack rolls. The damage bonus increases to +5.
##### Mercy
**Tag:** #Universal
When this feat affects a creature suffering a condition, you ease their affliction slightly.

- **Trained:** The target gains a +2 bonus to their next saving throw against the afflicting condition.
- **Expert:** The bonus increases to +4. 
- **Master:** The bonus increases to +6.

##### Sanctuary
**Tag:** #Aegis 
When this feat affects a creature, you shroud them in divine protection, granting an Aegis Bonus. This effect ends when the target is hit or missed by an attack.

- **Trained:** The target gains +3 Aegis Bonus to AC.
- **Expert:** The target gains +4 Aegis Bonus to AC. 
- **Master:** The target gains +5 Aegis Bonus to  AC. The effect now persists for 2 attacks before ending.

##### Strength
**Tag:** #Aegis 
When this feat affects a creature, you grant them an Aegis Bonus. 
- **Trained:** Increase the base **Aegis Bonus** by +3. Aegis Bonus for AC are capped at +1.
- **Expert:** Increase the base **Aegis Bonus** by +5. Aegis Bonus for AC are capped at +2.
- **Master:** Increase the base **Aegis Bonus** by +8. Aegis Bonus for AC are capped at +3.

##### Wrath
**Tag:** #Strike 
When this feat affects a creature, add **Strike Dice** to the feat's damaging roll.
- **Trained:** Add 1 **Strike Dice.** 
- **Expert:** You reroll 1s and 2s with your **Strike Dice.** 
- **Master:** Add 2 **Strike Dice** instead of 1. 

##### Retribution
**Tag:** #Strike 
Creatures that take **Strike Dice** damage, take additional effects.
- **Trained:** The targets next attack is [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Hindered]].
- **Expert:** The target is [[01 Condition#Off-Guard|Off-Guard]].
- **Master:** The target gains weakness 5 to all damage until the end of its next turn.
#### Level 6+
##### Renewal
**Tag:** #Vitality  
When this feat affects a creature, the healing lingers.

- **Expert:** The target regains 1d4 [[Hit Point]] as [[Lesser Healing]] at the start of each of their turns 1 minute.
- **Master:** The healing increases to a 1d6 of [[Greater Healing]]. If the target is [[Bloodied]] when this source is applied, the healing is 1d8 instead of 1d6. The healing then reverts back to 1d6 if they lose the [[01 Condition#Bloodied (WIP)|Bloodied]] condition.
##### Cascading Grace
**Tag:** #Vitality  
When this feat heals a creature, a portion of that healing spreads to a nearby ally.

- **Expert:** One ally within 10 feet of the target regains Hit Points equal to your Wisdom modifier as [[Lesser Healing]].
- **Master:** The range increases to 20 feet and the healing increases to 1d6 + your wisdom modifier as [[Greater Healing]]. You may now choose up to 2 allies within range to receive the cascading healing.
##### Bolstered Rejuvenation 
**Tag:** #Vitality  
Once per turn, when this feat heals a creature, healing becomes more effective on them.

- **Expert:** The target receives a +4 healing bonus to all healing they receive until the start of their next turn.
- **Master:** This bonus increases to +8. All [[Greater and Lesser Healing|Lesser Healing]] is converted to [[Greater and Lesser Healing|Greater Healing]]. 
##### Persistence

**Tag:** #Universal
Once per scene, when this feat affects a creature, you may extend the duration of the triggering feat. This source has no effect on feats with #Instantaneous as their duration.

- **Expert:** Extend the duration by 2 rounds.
- **Master:** As Expert. Reduce the action of this feat by 1 (minimum of 1). This source can be used twice per scene instead of once.

##### Providence
**Tag:** #Universal
Once per scene, when this feat affects a creature, divine providence watches over them.

- **Expert:** Until the end of your or the target's next turn, if they fail or critically fail a d20 roll they may reroll it and must take the new result.
- **Master:** The target gains [[Favored]] on all saving throws until the end of your or the target's next turn. This source can be used twice per scene instead of once.
##### Bulwark

**Tag:** #Aegis  
Once per turn, when this feat affects a creature, you add an **Aegis Bonus** as resistance to a specific damage type until the start of your next turn.

- **Expert:** The target gains an **Aegis Bonus** of 3 as resistance to one damage type of your choice. 
- **Master:** The Aegis Bonus increases to 5 and you may choose two damage types instead of one. Additionally the target gains a [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]] to saving throws against effects that deal the chosen damage types.

#### Level 10+
##### Judgement

Twice per scene, when this feat affects a creature, mark one enemy you can see with a Mark of Judgement. The next ally to deal damage to that enemy gains an additional effect determined by your training.

- **Master:** The ally deals an additional **Strike Dice** of damage. The die type is determined by the feat used. The die step of all damage dice rolled against the marked enemy increases by one step.

##### Zeal

Twice per scene, when this feat affects a creature, the next time that creature deals damage before the end of its next turn, it or one ally within 15 feet regains [[Hit Points]]

- **Master:** The chosen creature regains 3d8 [[Hit Points]] as [[Greater and Lesser Healing|Greater Healing]]
##### Consecration

Twice per scene, when this feat affects a creature, you sanctify the ground around them.

- **Master:** Create a 15-foot consecrated area centered on the target until the end of its next turn. Allies within the area gain a +4 bonus to AC and resistance 7 to all damage types.

### Martyr Domain
#### Level 2+

##### Vulnerability

Once per scene, when this feat affects a creature, you weaken yourself to increase your potency.

- **Trained:** You take a −2 penalty to AC until the start of your next turn. The feat's attack roll or spell save DC increases by 2.
- **Expert:** The AC penalty increases to −3 and the bonus increases to 3. This source can be used twice per scene instead of once.
- **Master:** The AC penalty increases to −4 and the bonus increases to 4. Additionally the target cannot benefit from resistance to the feat's damage type until the start of your next turn.

##### Sacrifice of Mind

Once per scene, when this feat affects a creature, you reduce your own mental capacity to empower yourself.

- **Trained:** All your wisdom d20 rolls are [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Hindered]]. You, or the target of this feat, has their next, Strength, Dexterity or Constitution rolls [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]]. Once this [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]] roll is used, or 10 minutes have past (whichever happens first), your wisdom rolls are no longer [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Hindered]].
- **Expert:** This source can be used twice per scene instead of once. Additionally the [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Favored]] now applies to the next two Strength, Dexterity, or Constitution rolls.
- **Master:** When the [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Hindered]] effect ends, you regain [[Hit Points]] equal to your Wisdom modifier × 2
