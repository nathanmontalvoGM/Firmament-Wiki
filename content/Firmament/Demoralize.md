With a sudden shout, a well-timed taunt, or a cutting put-down, you can shake an enemy's resolve. Choose a creature within 30 feet of you who you're aware of. Attempt an intimidation check against that target's Wisdom DC. If the target doesn't understand the language you are speaking you are [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Hindered]] on the roll. Subsequent attempts to demoralize within the next 10 minutes are [[Favored and Hindered (Advantage System)|Hindered]]. 

**Critical Success.** the target is [[01 Condition#Frightened|Frightened]].
**Success.** the target is [[01 Condition#Dazzled|Dazzled]].
