#### Momentum
##### Swift - Minor
You can move while landing attacks. 

Trained - Once per turn when you hit with a weapon you are trained with, you can reposition yourself by 10 feet.
Expert - As trained but your movement increases to 15 feet and you ignore impeded movement
Master - This effect is not limited to once per turn and can be done as many times as you hit. The target is also off-guard to subsequent attacks after the first until the start of your next turn.  
Legendary - You ignore strained movement as well as any movement impeding effects (except those that would reduce your speed to 0). You also move up to your full speed.

##### Rapid - Severe
Works with MAP. You reduce MAP on your attacks  with this feat  by 1 and 2 (meaning you will roll at -4 on your second attack, and -8 on your third). This stacks with the agile weapon charactertic, resulting in a -3 and -6 respectively. 

Master - your MAP becomes -4 and -8. 
Legendary - Additional effect not related to MAP. 

##### Reaction (Name sucks here) - Minor
Attacks that use your reaction are more powerful

Trained - attacks of opportunity deal an additional [[Weapon Dice|Weapon Die]] of damage. 
Expert  - You can make an attack of opportunity without an enemy triggering it. Attacking in this way takes a -4 penalty to the roll. 
Master - add two additional [[Weapon Dice]] of damage to your opportunity attacks. 
Legendary - This attack gains Favored. Getting a critical hit with this attack increases the die step of all damage dice rolled by one step (maximum of 1d12)

##### Deft Hands - Major
You can perform additional actions as a free action once per turn after you hit with an attack. You gain a bonus to this roll determined by your level. These actions incur MAP as normal. 

Expert - You can attempt to disarm the creature. You gain a +2 bonus to the roll.
Master - You can attempt to trip the creature. You gain a +4 bonus to the roll
Legendary - You can attempt to do something else. You gain Favored on this roll.

##### Sweeping
You can make second attack on a creature within range once per turn. This attack incurs MAP. 

Trained - Make a second attack as part of the first attack against a creature within 5 feet of the original target. This attack incurs MAP and you take -2 penalty to the damage roll on this second attack.
