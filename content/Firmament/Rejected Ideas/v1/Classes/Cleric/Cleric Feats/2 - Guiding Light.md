- **Prerequisite:** [[Life Domain]] 
- **Level:** 2
- **Tag:** #Vitality

Your presence is an inspiration to others, saving them in times of peril. You can cast guidance as a reaction a number of times equal to your spellcasting ability regaining all spent uses on a [[Long Rest]]. Also, guidance cast in this way can be added to attack rolls in addition to ability checks.
